## Reaching the Third Sea: The Red Emperor Challenge

To access the new content, players must first prove their worth by transitioning from the Second Sea. This isn't a simple travel quest; it involves a gatekeeper and a legendary confrontation. According to player experience, the Gatekeeper acts as the final barrier, warning players that the path forward is guarded by the "Red Emperor"—the formidable Shanks.

Unlike many other bosses in the game that can be tackled with a crew, the fight to enter the Third Sea is a "solo dolo" mission. You must face Shanks alone in a dedicated arena. Community reports suggest that Shanks hits incredibly hard, often referred to as "hitting like a truck," but he does have a health-saving mechanic. If you die during the fight, he may stay at his current health level, allowing you to retry and chip away at him until he is defeated.

| Step | Action | Requirements/Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Locate the Gatekeeper | Found at the edge of the Second Sea. |
| 2 | Accept the Challenge | You must be prepared for a solo boss fight. |
| 3 | Defeat Shanks | Use high-damage combos like Metal Roar and Claw Barrage. |
| 4 | Enter the Portal | Once defeated, a cutscene plays, and you are transported to the Third Sea. |

## Master of Blades: Three Sword Style (3SS) V1 and V2

One of the most exciting aspects of this update is the expansion of the Three Sword Style (3SS). For players who have been grinding their mastery, the transition from V1 to V2 offers a significant power spike. However, the path to obtaining these styles is steep and requires a combination of rare swords and high mastery levels.

To obtain 3SS V1, you must possess three specific swords: Sand, Enma, and Shisui. Each of these must be leveled to 300 mastery. Once you meet these criteria, you can visit a specific rock formation in the world to unlock the style. The V2 version, however, requires even more dedication. Players must seek out Doryan the Forge and provide rare materials to upgrade their blades.

### 3SS V2 Requirements and Moves
According to community reports, V2 moves are inspired by the Wano-style techniques seen in popular anime. The visual effects are significantly enhanced, featuring swirling energy and massive area-of-effect (AoE) impact.

| Style Version | Requirement 1 | Requirement 2 | Requirement 3 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **3SS V1** | Sand Sword (300 Mastery) | Enma (300 Mastery) | Shisui (300 Mastery) |
| **3SS V2** | 3SS V1 (300 Mastery) | 5 Ancient Blade Relics | Talk to Doryan the Forge |

The move set for 3SS V2 is designed for high-tier PvP and boss raiding. The "Soaring Phoenix" provides excellent range, while "3,000 Skies" acts as a devastating finisher that can clear out entire waves of NPCs.

### 3SS V2 Move Set Breakdown
*   **Soaring Phoenix:** A ranged slash with enhanced Wano-inspired effects.
*   **Purgatory Descent:** A high-impact downward strike that freezes enemies in place.
*   **3,000 Skies:** A flurry of slashes that covers a wide radius, perfect for crowd control.

## The Mythical Okuchi Fruit: Powers of the Frost

The Haze Seas update introduces a new pinnacle of power: the Mythical Okuchi Fruit. Based on the legendary weapon and form of Yamato, this fruit offers a blend of ice and fire (Frozen Flames) that provides both utility and raw damage. It is a "transformation" type fruit, meaning its true potential is unlocked when the player enters their hybrid form.

In its base form, the Okuchi fruit allows for quick M1 (basic attack) strings and cold-based projectiles. Once transformed, the user becomes much larger and more "vicious," with enhanced speed and damage. Player experience indicates that the "Cold Breath" move is particularly effective in the Third Sea for stunning fast-moving bosses.

| Move Name | Effect | Transformation Bonus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Feral Pursuit** | A dash-and-grab move that closes distance. | Becomes "Feral Strike" with higher impact. |
| **Frozen Flames** | Inflicts both burn and freeze status effects. | Increased AoE and duration. |
| **Cold Breath** | A wide-angle frost spray that stuns enemies. | Becomes "Blistering Fury," adding fire damage. |
| **Seeking Serpent** | A tracking projectile that follows the target. | Becomes "Pursuing Serpent" with two follow-up slices. |

## New Systems: Race Storage and Summonable Minions

Beyond combat, the update introduces several "Quality of Life" systems that players have requested for months. The most notable is the Race Store and Fruit Store. Previously, spinning for a new race meant losing your current one forever. Now, the "Archivist of Bloodline" allows you to store your current race in a slot, preserving it even if you decide to roll for something else.

### Storage and Gems
Storage slots are not free; they require Gems, which are a premium currency earned through gameplay or [official Roblox](https://www.roblox.com) events. Each slot costs approximately 1,000 Gems, and players can unlock up to five slots for both races and fruits. This allows for much more flexible "build-swapping" depending on whether you are focusing on PvP or farming.

### The Demon Minion
A unique feature in this update is the ability to summon a "Demon Boss" as a minion. To unlock this, players must reach a staggering 25 million bounty. Once this milestone is hit, you receive a Demon Spawner. Community reports suggest that this giant blob-like creature follows the player and assists in combat against other players, though its effectiveness against standard NPCs is currently being debated among the community.

Crafting these requires "Sea Beast Hearts," which are rare drops from sea encounters.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I get to the 3rd Sea in the Haze Seas update?
To reach the 3rd Sea, you must find the Gatekeeper in the 2nd Sea. He will challenge you to a solo boss fight against Shanks (the Red Emperor). Once you defeat him, you will view a cutscene and be granted access to the new Third Sea map.

### What is the best fruit in the Haze Seas update?
Currently, the Mythical Okuchi fruit is considered one of the best due to its high damage and hybrid transformation. It offers both freezing and burning effects, making it versatile for both boss fights and player-versus-player (PvP) encounters.

### How do I get Three Sword Style V2?
To unlock 3SS V2, you must first have 300 mastery on the V1 version. You then need to collect 5 Ancient Blade Relics and take them to Doryan the Forge. This will upgrade your moves to the Wano-inspired variants like Soaring Phoenix and 3,000 Skies.

### What are the benefits of the Sea Beast Armor V2?
The Sea Beast Armor V2 provides a massive boost of 3,500 Health and a 10% increase in movement speed. When paired with the Helmet V2, it also grants an additional level of Observation Haki and 150 extra Stamina, making it the premier gear set for the Haze Seas update endgame.
