## Understanding the Fruit Economy: Can You Directly Sell Fruits?

In many Roblox pirate games, players expect a direct "sell" button where they can hand over physical fruits to an NPC in exchange for instant cash. In this game, however, the economy is structured differently. 

According to player experience and community reports on the [Haze Piece Wiki Fandom](https://haze-piece.fandom.com/wiki/Fruit_Dealer), you cannot directly sell physical fruits back to an NPC for Beli (money) once they are in your inventory. Instead, "selling" or managing your fruits is done through replacement, trading within the community, or optimizing your rolls at the Fruit Dealer. 

If you are looking at **haze seas how to sell fruits**, your primary focus should be on inventory management: keeping high-value fruits, replacing low-tier ones, and rolling the gacha system efficiently.

### Fruit Disposal and Replacement Rules

Before you make a mistake and lose a mythical power, you must understand how the game handles equipped fruits:

*   **Overwriting:** Eating a new fruit or purchasing one directly from the shop stock will automatically replace your currently equipped fruit.
*   **No Refunds:** Fruits bought with Beli are expendable. When you overwrite them, they are gone forever.
*   **Robux Permanence:** If you purchase a fruit using Robux, it is permanently unlocked, allowing you to equip it again later without losing it.

## Navigating the Fruit Dealer NPC

The Fruit Dealer is the central hub for all fruit transactions in the game. While you cannot sell your physical items to him, he is the key to spending your hard-earned Beli, Gems, or Robux to acquire better powers. 

### Gacha vs. Stock System

The dealer offers two main methods to obtain fruits. Understanding these systems is vital for managing your resources:

1.  **The Gacha System:** You roll for a random fruit. This costs 125,000 Beli, 50 Gems, or 200 Robux. There is a strict limit of **5 rolls every 12 hours** when using Beli. Robux and Gem rolls bypass this cooldown.
2.  **The Stock System:** The dealer has a rotating stock of specific fruits that changes every 2 hours. Common and Uncommon fruits are always in stock, while Rare, Legendary, and Mythical fruits have low spawn chances.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you sell fruits for Beli in Haze Seas?
No, there is currently no direct way to sell physical fruits to an NPC for Beli. If you are looking up **haze seas how to sell fruits**, the only options are to overwrite your current fruit by eating a new one, or to manage your inventory by purchasing permanent versions with Robux.

### What is the cooldown for the Fruit Dealer's gacha?
When rolling for fruits using Beli (125k per roll), you are limited to 5 rolls. Once you hit this limit, you must wait 12 hours for the cooldown to reset. You can bypass this cooldown entirely by using Gems or Robux.

### How often does the Fruit Dealer's stock change?
The dealer's stock rotates every 2 hours. Common and Uncommon fruits are always available, while Rare, Legendary, and Mythical fruits have a random chance to appear with each rotation.

### Do I lose my equipped fruit if I buy a new one?
Yes. If you buy a fruit with Beli or eat a physical fruit from your inventory, it will automatically overwrite your currently equipped power. The old fruit is lost permanently unless you originally purchased it with Robux, which allows for permanent re-equipping.
