## Overview

Fruits are one of the most important Haze Seas decisions because they affect how comfortable your farming, fighting, and progression feel. A fruit should not be judged only by rarity. For most players, the better question is whether the fruit is useful for your current goal.

When comparing fruits, use four simple checks: grinding speed, boss fight usefulness, mobility, and ease of use. A fruit that feels strong for experienced players may not be the best beginner choice if it is difficult to control or depends on specific timing.

### Fruit Comparison Cards

#### Grinding Fruits

#### Boss Fight Fruits

Boss-focused fruits should be evaluated by how well they help players survive, move, and keep pressure during longer fights. Do not claim exact boss performance unless the data is verified.
