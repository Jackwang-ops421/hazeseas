## How the Haze Seas Fruit Stock System Works

The Fruit Dealer is an essential NPC in Haze Seas (formerly known as Haze Piece). Instead of relying purely on random spawns under trees or expensive gacha spins, players can purchase specific fruits directly from the dealer's rotating inventory. 

However, you cannot simply buy any fruit at any time. The dealer's inventory changes on a strict real-time schedule. Here are the core rules of the stock system based on player experience and community reports:

*   **Permanent Stock:** Common and Uncommon fruits are always available. They do not rotate out, so you never have to worry about missing them.
*   **Rotating Stock:** Rare, Legendary, and Mythical fruits rotate in and out of the shop randomly with each reset.
*   **Global Sync:** The stock is identical across all servers worldwide. If a fruit is in stock on an eastern US server, it is also in stock on a Tokyo server.
*   **Pricing Options:** Every fruit can be purchased using either in-game Money (Beli) or premium Robux. Buying with Robux permanently unlocks the fruit.

To make sure you are prepared, keep an eye on the official [Roblox Home Page](https://www.roblox.com) to ensure your account has enough Robux, or farm Sea Beasts to keep your in-game wallet full.

## Tips for Managing Your Fruit Stock Purchases

Successfully securing the best abilities from the **haze seas fruit stock** requires a mix of preparation and patience. Here are some pro tips from veteran players:

### 1. Keep a Financial Reserve
Never let your in-game money drop to zero. S-tier fruits are incredibly expensive when they roll into the shop. Keep a baseline of Beli saved up so you can instantly purchase a legendary fruit if it appears during an odd hour of the day.

### 2. Prioritize Farming Fruits First

Once you reach the max level, you can transition to PvP-focused fruits like **Electricity** or **Shadow**.

### 3. Coordinate with the Community
Since the stock is global, join active community Discord servers or guild chats. Players frequently ping stock updates the moment a highly sought-after fruit like **Dragon** or **Phoenix** hits the rotating shop, giving you plenty of time to log in and buy it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often does the haze seas fruit stock reset?
The stock resets every 2 hours globally. The exact time depends on your local time zone, but the rotation schedule remains constant across all servers worldwide.

### Why can't I see Mythical fruits in the stock?
Common and Uncommon fruits are permanently in stock, but Rare, Legendary, and Mythical fruits only have a small percentage chance to appear with each 2-hour reset. You have to check back regularly or rely on community alerts to catch them.

### Is it better to buy fruits with Money or Robux from the stock?
If you buy a fruit with in-game Money, it will be replaced and lost if you consume another fruit later. However, buying a fruit with Robux permanently unlocks it on your account, allowing you to equip it again at any time for free from the dealer.

### Does the stock change during special in-game events?
Yes. During major holiday events or game updates, the developers occasionally force-update the stock or increase the spawn rates of high-tier fruits. Make sure to save your currency ahead of announced game updates!
