## How to Obtain the Dragon Fruit

The Dragon Fruit is classified as a Mythical Beast type, placing it at the absolute pinnacle of rarity. Because of its game-changing damage output and defensive utility, getting your hands on it requires either immense luck or a significant hoard of in-game currency.

| Acquisition Method | Details & Requirements |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Fruit Dealer NPC** | Costs 5,000,000 Cash or 2,999 Robux (Tied with Dough as the most expensive fruit) |
| **Gacha / Roll** | 0.25% chance to roll (Tied with Dough as the rarest fruit in the game) |
| **Trading** | High demand in the player economy; typically requires multiple legendary items |

If you want to customize your draconic appearance, you can spin for alternative dragon colors. According to player reports, this can be done in the Second Sea at Hydra Island by speaking to the Striker Boat NPC for a cost of 100 Gems per spin.

## The Reworked Moveset: Base, Beast, and Hybrid Forms

The developers completely overhauled the **haze seas dragon fruit**, introducing a highly versatile Hybrid Form alongside the classic Full Beast Form. This update ensures that players have specialized tools for both large-scale mob farming and fast-paced single-target PvP encounters.

### Base / Untransformed Moveset

Before you unlock your major transformations, your human form utilizes basic fiery projectiles and quick physical strikes to build up early mastery points.


## Step-by-Step Leveling Strategy with Dragon

Grinding to max level with the **haze seas dragon fruit** is an absolute breeze if you allocate your stats correctly. Because the fruit's base damage scales incredibly well, you should prioritize your offensive stats early on.

### Phase 1: First Sea Progression (Levels 1 - 2,200)

When starting out, immediately dump all of your attribute points into the **Fruit** stat. This ensures your early skills, like *Bullet Barrage*, can one-shot standard quest mobs.

1.  **Set Your Spawn Points:** Always set your spawn at the active quest island. Since you will be running a high-damage, low-HP build, occasional deaths to bosses might happen.
2.  **Gather Mobs:** Use your flight or basic attacks to aggro 4 to 5 enemies, group them up, and unleash *Bullet Barrage* or *Dragon's Ascent*.
3.  **Unlock Dragon Bite:** Once you hit 50 Mastery, use *Dragon Bite* to lock down high-health mini-bosses.
4.  **AFK Mastery Grinding:** To quickly unlock your transformations, player experience suggests setting up an auto-clicker overnight to cycle through your unlocked skills on low-level enemies.

| Level Range | Recommended Island / Quest | Key Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 - 100 | Starter Island | Focus entirely on *Bullet Barrage* and *Dragon's Ascent*. |
| 100 - 1,500 | Desert & Marine Base | Use *Dragon Exhale* to snipe enemies from long range. |
| 1,500 - 2,200 | Volcano & Sky Islands | Group up high-level mobs and clear them instantly with Full Beast transformations. |

## Transitioning to the Third Sea

Once you surpass level 3,200 and complete your journey through the Second Sea, you will gain access to the challenging Third Sea. At this stage of the game, enemies hit incredibly hard, and simple button-mashing will no longer suffice.

### Third Sea Stat Allocation

At high levels, you must balance your glass-cannon offense with enough survivability to withstand ultimate attacks from late-game bosses.

```
[Max Level Stats: 60% Fruit Damage / 30% Defense (HP) / 10% Melee (Stamina)]
```

By utilizing the Hybrid Form in the Third Sea, your M1 attacks become incredibly fast and fluid. You can easily weave basic physical attacks between your skill cooldowns. If a boss launches a massive, unavoidable area-of-effect attack, simply press your transformation key to revert to human form, instantly dodging the incoming projectile with your reduced hitbox.

The **haze seas dragon fruit** remains an undisputed top-tier choice for both casual players and competitive grinders. Its design provides several distinct mechanical advantages:

*   **Infinite Flight:** Simplifies map navigation and allows you to cheese difficult bosses by hovering just out of their melee range.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the drop rate of the haze seas dragon fruit?
The Dragon Fruit is a Mythical Beast fruit with an incredibly low roll chance of just 0.25% from the Fruit Dealer. Alternatively, it can be purchased directly when it is in stock for 5,000,000 Cash or 2,999 Robux.

### Can you use swords or fighting styles while transformed?
When you are in your Full Beast or Hybrid dragon forms, your standard fighting styles, swords, and guns are disabled. Reverting to your human form instantly restores your ability to use these weapons.

### How do I change the color of my dragon transformation?
You can change your dragon's color by traveling to Hydra Island in the Second Sea. Speak to the Striker Boat NPC and spend 100 Gems to spin for a new cosmetic color.

### Is the Dragon Fruit better than Kitsune for grinding?
While Kitsune offers incredible single-target speed and burst damage, the **haze seas dragon fruit** is widely considered superior for mob farming and sea beast hunting due to its massive, sweeping hitboxes and passive flight capabilities.
