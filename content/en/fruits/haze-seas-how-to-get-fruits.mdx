## 1. The Core Methods of Obtaining Fruits

There are several pathways to securing Devil Fruits in the game. Players can choose to spend their time hunting, saving in-game currency, or using premium options depending on their preferred playstyle. 

### Natural Spawns (The Free Hunter Method)
For players who do not want to spend Beli or Robux, natural world spawns are a highly viable option. Fruits spawn on the ground under random trees across the map.
* **Spawn Interval:** Exactly every 60 minutes (1 hour) on public servers.
* **Despawn Timer:** If a spawned fruit is not picked up within 20 minutes of appearing, it will despawn.
* **Pro-Tip:** Purchasing the **Fruit Notifier Gamepass** for 2,700 Robux will alert you when a fruit spawns and show you its exact location, making passive hunting incredibly efficient.

### The Devil Fruit Dealer (Cynthia)
Located centrally at the Starter Island, the Devil Fruit Dealer (Cynthia) is the most reliable NPC for active progression. Here, you have two primary options:
1. **Rolling/Spinning:** You can roll a random fruit by paying 125,000 Beli, 50 Gems, or 200 Robux. 
2. **Direct Stock Purchases:** The dealer’s stock rotates regularly. If a fruit you want is currently in stock, you can purchase it directly using Beli or Robux.

### Sea Events & Community Drops
According to player experience and community reports on the [Roblox Platform](https://www.roblox.com), participating in high-level Sea Events can reward players with random fruit drops. Additionally, generous high-level players frequently drop unwanted fruits on the ground in starter areas, so keep your eyes peeled when visiting populated docks.

## 5. Tips to Maximize Your Fruit Hunting Efficiency

If you want to optimize your search and secure the best fruits as fast as possible, keep these community-tested strategies in mind:

* **Server Hopping:** Because fruits spawn every 60 minutes on public servers, hopping between different servers can help you find worlds where a fruit has recently spawned but hasn't been claimed yet.
* **Redeem Promo Codes:** The developers regularly release active codes that grant free Gems and Beli. Use these free resources to roll at the Devil Fruit Dealer without having to grind mobs for hours.
* **Keep an Eye on the Despawn Clock:** If you are actively hunting without a game pass, remember that you only have a 20-minute window to locate a fruit once it spawns. Focus your search around dense clusters of trees on major islands.
* **Save for Permanent Options:** If you find a fruit you absolutely love, saving up Robux to buy its permanent version ensures you never lose it, even if you decide to eat a different fruit to try out a new build.

## FAQ: Haze Seas How to Get Fruits

### How do I get fruits in Haze Seas without spending Robux?
To get fruits without Robux, you can claim your one-time free community fruit, hunt for natural spawns under trees every 60 minutes, or buy rolls from the Devil Fruit Dealer using 125,000 Beli or 50 Gems earned through gameplay.

### What is the spawn rate for Devil Fruits in public servers?
Fruits spawn on the ground under random trees every 60 minutes. Once spawned, players have exactly 20 minutes to find and collect the fruit before it automatically despawns from the world.

### Can I lose my equipped fruit if I eat a new one?
Yes. If you consume a new Devil Fruit, your previously equipped fruit will be overwritten and permanently lost, unless you purchased the "Permanent" version of that fruit using Robux.

### Where is the Devil Fruit Dealer located?
The Devil Fruit Dealer NPC (Cynthia) is located in the central town area of the Starter Island. This is the primary hub where players go to buy, roll, and trade their fruits.
