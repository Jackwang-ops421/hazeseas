## FAQ: Haze Seas Dough Fruit

### How rare is the Dough Fruit in Haze Seas?
The Dough Fruit is a Mythical rarity fruit. It has a 0.25% chance of being rolled from the Fruit Dealer and a 0.5% chance of appearing in the shop's stock. Because of these low odds, many players consider it one of the hardest fruits to obtain without using Robux.

### Where can I find the Dough Boss?
The Super Boss Dough User is located in the Second Sea on Doughnut Island. This boss is intended for high-level players (typically level 2,000 and above) and drops valuable items required for endgame progression.

### Is the haze seas dough fruit good for grinding?
Yes, the **haze seas dough fruit** is excellent for grinding because of its "Dough Minigun" skill, which can hit multiple enemies at once. Its "Water Walk" passive also makes navigating between islands much safer and faster than other fruits.

### What is the best stat build for a Dough user?
Most players recommend putting the majority of your points into the "Fruit" category to maximize skill damage. You should also put enough points into "Stamina" to ensure you can use your mobility moves frequently and "Defense" to survive hits from high-level bosses.

### Can I walk on water with the Dough Fruit?
Yes, one of the unique passive abilities of the Dough Fruit is the Water Walk. This allows you to stand and move on the surface of the ocean without taking the typical "fruit user" drowning damage, making it one of the best fruits for sea exploration.
