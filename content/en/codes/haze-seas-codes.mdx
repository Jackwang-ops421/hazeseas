Haze Seas is a high-fidelity Roblox RPG inspired by the world-renowned *One Piece* anime. Players are dropped into a vast ocean filled with islands, each containing unique quests, NPCs, and secrets. The core gameplay loop revolves around finding "Devil Fruits"—mystical fruits that grant superhuman powers at the cost of the ability to swim.

### Exploring the World
The map in Haze Seas is divided into different "Seas" or tiers. As you level up using your **haze seas codes**, you will move from the Starter Island to more dangerous territories like Marine Base or the Desert Kingdom. Each area features a boss that drops unique loot, ranging from swords to cosmetic accessories that provide stat buffs.

### Building Your Character
Character progression is handled through a stat point system. Every time you level up, you earn points to invest in:
*   **Melee:** Increases punch damage and stamina.
*   **Sword:** Enhances damage dealt with katanas and sabers.
*   **Defense:** Increases your total health pool.
*   **Fruit:** Boosts the power of your Devil Fruit abilities.

According to veteran players, a "balanced" build is often weaker than a "specialized" build. If you find a powerful fruit early on using your free Gems, it is often better to dump the majority of your points into the Fruit stat to maximize your area-of-effect (AoE) damage.
