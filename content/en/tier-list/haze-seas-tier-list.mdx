## Haze Seas Fruit Tier List: Best Devil Fruits Ranked

Fruits in Haze Seas function similarly to those in legendary Roblox titles like *Blox Fruits*, serving as the cornerstone of most player builds. To help you choose, we have classified every fruit from S-Tier down to D-Tier based on their damage output, area-of-effect (AoE) capabilities, mobility, and overall utility.

| Tier | Fruit Name | Primary Focus | Summary of Performance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **S-Tier** | Electricity, Dragon, Dough, Phoenix, Shadow, Gum | PvP & PvE Meta | Elite damage, massive AoE, excellent mobility, and great scalability. |
| **A-Tier** | Darkness, Light, Magma, Venom, Leopard, Wolf, Tremor, Okuchi | Sub-Meta | Extremely powerful but often require high mastery levels or transformations. |
| **B-Tier** | Gas, Snow, String, Buddha, Mammoth, Gravity, Flame, Love, Soul, Ice, Magnet | Niche / Alternative | Solid mid-game choices; great for specific tasks like tanking or travel. |
| **C-Tier** | Barrier, Paw, Sand, Operation, Smoke | Early Game Only | Decent for starter islands but quickly outclassed as you progress. |
| **D-Tier** | Circus, Bomb, Spike, Spin, Vanish | Avoid / Replace | Low damage ceilings, minimal utility, and lackluster scaling. |

### S-Tier Fruits

These are the absolute best fruits in the game, offering unmatched versatility for both farming and combat.

*   **Electricity:** Widely considered by the community as the premier fruit for grinding. Its moves come out lightning-fast, deal massive damage, and boast incredible AoE ranges.
*   **Dragon:** Following recent reworks, Dragon stands at the top of the meta. While some players report that its hitboxes can occasionally feel janky, its move-spam potential makes it a monster in both PvP and PvE.
*   **Dough:** Features incredible range and crowd control. Skills like *Grilled Mochi* and *Dough Minigun* clear out clustered groups of enemies in seconds.
*   **Phoenix:** One of the most versatile choices. It offers exceptional flight for map traversal, a high-damage single-target dash, and the devastating *Heavenly Twin Tornadoes* for group clearing.
*   **Shadow:** Exceptional for keeping enemies at bay. *Bat Swarm* creates passive damage-over-time zones, while *Bat Fly* gives you crucial invincibility frames (i-frames) to dodge heavy boss attacks.
*   **Gum:** A highly balanced choice that excels in single-target and AoE damage, especially once you unlock its powerful Gear transformations.

### A-Tier Fruits

These fruits are incredibly strong but might require a bit more setup, high mastery investment, or specific playstyles to unlock their full potential.

*   **Light & Magma:** Both are legendary for progression. Magma allows you to walk on water (immensely helpful for sea exploration), while Light offers the fastest travel speed in the game. However, their top-tier damage moves are locked behind high mastery levels.
*   **Okuchi:** A highly reliable transformation fruit that performs excellently in PvP, though it lags slightly behind Dragon for raw PvE clearing power.
*   **Darkness:** Offers some of the best crowd control in the game with *Dark Vortex* and *Black Hole*, grouping enemies together perfectly for sword or fighting style follow-ups.
*   **Venom:** Features great AoE and defensive options, but its slow projectile speeds make it easier for experienced PvP opponents to dodge.

### B-Tier Fruits

B-Tier fruits are respectable options that can carry you through the mid-game. 

*   **Buddha:** The ultimate tank. While it completely lacks mobility skills, its massive defense boost makes it the safest choice for soloing difficult PvE bosses.
*   **Magnet:** Boasts incredible burst damage with its *Railgun* ability, but slow charge-up animations leave you highly vulnerable in fast-paced PvP fights.
*   **Ice:** A fantastic utility fruit. It allows you to walk on water and freeze enemies, making it a favorite for starter sea-faring players.

## Haze Seas Sword Tier List: Best Melee Weapons

Swords are essential for hybrid builds and players who prefer fast-paced, slash-based combat. Obtaining the best swords usually requires defeating difficult bosses or completing complex crafting recipes in the later seas.

| **Dual Dark Blade** | **S+** | Ultimate PvP & PvE | Craft at Doran the Forge (Sea 3): Requires 20 Ancient Blade Relics, 25M Bounty, 300 Mastery on Dark Blade V2, 1 Abyssal Eye, and $20M. |
| **Dark Blade V2** | **S** | High DPS & Speed | Sea 2: Sacrifice a Legendary+ Fruit to Nikk0lapz in Foggy Castle, defeat the 3SS Boss, then pay 50 Gems to spawn and defeat the Dark Blade Boss. |

### How to Choose Your Sword
According to player feedback, players focusing purely on PvP should prioritize **Gryphon** or **Dark Blade V2** due to their rapid animations and easy-to-chain combos. For raw PvE boss-melting, the **Dual Dark Blade** remains the undisputed king of the seas, though its hefty crafting requirements make it a true end-game goal.

Your character's race provides passive stat boosts and unique active abilities that can dramatically alter your combat effectiveness. Upgrading your race to V2 is highly recommended once you reach Sea 2.


However, if you cannot roll D. Clan, **Demon** is an incredibly strong alternative. The life steal passive provides superb sustain during long, drawn-out boss battles, allowing you to stay in the fight without relying on defensive fruits.

## How to Get and Remove Fruits in Haze Seas

Acquiring the fruits ranked high on our **haze seas tier list** requires knowing where to look. Players can acquire fruits through several in-game methods:

1.  **Cynthia the Fruit Dealer (Gacha):** You can spend Gems or Beli to spin for random fruits at the Starter Island or the Level 350 Island.
2.  **Natural Spawns:** Fruits randomly spawn under random trees across the map every 60 minutes.
3.  **Free Community Chest:** Liking the game on Roblox and joining the official developer group awards a free chest on the starter island.
4.  **Trading:** Engage with the active community to trade your spare fruits for ones you need.

If you eat a fruit and want to change your build, visit **Doctor White** (located on the left side of the Level 350 Island) to safely remove your current fruit. You can learn more about the game's mechanics and world map on the [Roblox Platform](https://www.roblox.com) where the game is hosted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the absolute best fruit in the haze seas tier list?
For overall utility, farming, and grinding, **Electricity** is currently ranked as the best fruit due to its rapid attack animations and massive AoE damage. For high-end PvP and raw power scaling, **Dragon** and **Dough** share the top spot.

### How do I reroll my race in Haze Seas?
You can reroll your race by using active promo codes, claiming daily login rewards, participating in random boss drops, or using the AFK Chamber located on the Level 350 Island.

### Is the Dark Blade worth the grind?
Yes, absolutely. The **Dark Blade V2** and its upgraded form, the **Dual Dark Blade**, are the highest-performing swords in the game. They offer unmatched attack speed and burst damage, making them top priorities for any sword-main build on our **haze seas tier list**.

### Can I survive in the water if I have a Devil Fruit?
Normally, eating a fruit makes you take damage when touching seawater. However, if you roll the **Fishborne** race and upgrade it to V2, you will gain complete immunity to water damage, allowing you to swim freely even as a fruit user.
