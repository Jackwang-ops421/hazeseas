## Locating the Sea Beast: The Rocky Pillars

The Sea Beast is a Level 700 Superboss that spawns in a specific high-level zone. To find this aquatic reptile, you must navigate your ship to the Rocky Pillars. This region is characterized by towering stone columns rising directly out of the ocean, creating a treacherous arena for naval combat. 

When navigating to the Rocky Pillars, look for the Crew Flag placed in the area. The Sea Beast spawns in the immediate vicinity of this flag. Because of the boss's high level and massive damage output, players should ensure they are properly leveled and geared before sailing into these waters. Community reports suggest that while the boss is Level 700, its high damage scaling makes it a threat even to higher-level adventurers who fail to dodge its attacks.

## Mastering the Sea Beast's Attack Patterns

The Sea Beast utilizes a highly predictable, consecutive attack loop consisting of three distinct moves. Because the boss uses these moves in a fixed order (from attack A to attack C), you can anticipate its next action and position yourself accordingly. 

Farming for a **haze seas sea beast core** requires a deep understanding of these sequential attack patterns to minimize damage. The table below outlines each of the three attacks, their damage values, and how to counter them.

| Attack Name | Order | Damage | Special Effects | Counter Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aquatic Fireball** | First (A) | 2,500 | Large AoE hitbox | Watch for the telegraphed windup; dash laterally to escape the blast zone. |
| **Roar** | Second (B) | 0 | Stuns & disables Observation Haki | Anticipate the stun; prepare to reactivate Observation Haki immediately after the roar ends. |
| **Sea Beam** | Third (C) | 1,500 | Fast, long-range linear projectile | Small hitbox but short windup; use your reactivated Haki or dodge quickly to the side. |

### Tactical Breakdown of the Cycle

Because the Sea Beast always executes its attacks in the sequence of **Aquatic Fireball -> Roar -> Sea Beam**, you can plan your defensive maneuvers in advance. 

For example, when the Sea Beast prepares its Aquatic Fireball, you should focus entirely on movement to avoid the massive 2,500-damage explosion. As soon as the fireball lands, prepare yourself for the Roar. Since the Roar deals no damage but stuns you and disables your Observation Haki, you must wait out the stun and immediately turn your Observation Haki back on. Doing so ensures you are protected against the rapid Sea Beam that follows, which deals 1,500 damage and can easily catch unprepared players off guard.

## Solo and Team Combat Strategies

Defeating a Level 700 Superboss is no small feat. While the developer likely intended for players to tackle this boss in coordinated teams, the gaming community has developed several strategies to defeat the beast both legitimately and through clever positioning.

### The Team Approach (Legitimate Strategy)

If you are fighting the Sea Beast as a crew, communication is key. Because the boss deals high single-target and area-of-effect damage, players should rotate aggro. 

While one player draws the focus of the Aquatic Fireball, other players should position themselves behind or to the side of the beast to unleash their highest-damage skills. Having at least one player dedicated to calling out the attack cycle (Fireball, Roar, Beam) can drastically improve the survival rate of the entire team.

### The Solo "Cheese" Strategy

If you do not have a crew or prefer to farm solo, there is a widely known community method to defeat the Sea Beast without taking damage. 

Near the Rocky Pillars, there is a specific stone column where the Sky Walk Trainer NPC resides. By climbing to the top of this pillar, you can position yourself out of reach of the Sea Beast's short-range attacks. From this safe vantage point, you can continuously spam long-range projectile moves, magic skills, or gun attacks to slowly chip away at the boss's health pool. 

Whether you choose to fight legitimately or cheese the boss from the Sky Walk Trainer pillar, your ultimate goal remains the same: extracting the elusive **haze seas sea beast core**.
