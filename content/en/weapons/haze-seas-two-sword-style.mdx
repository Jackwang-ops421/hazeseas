## Unlocking the Basics: How to Get Two Sword Style V1

Before you can unleash massive ranged wind attacks, you must first master the basics of the style. The journey begins in Logue City (often referred to by players as Logue Town), a bustling hub filled with merchants, quests, and vital NPCs. 

To purchase the **haze seas two sword style** V1, you must locate an NPC named Doctor White. He is the sole vendor of this entry-level dual-wielding style. However, Doctor White does not hand over his training for free. You will need to bring a substantial amount of currency to secure his services. 

### Requirements for V1

*   **Location:** Logue City / Logue Town
*   **NPC Trainer:** Doctor White
*   **Cost:** $100,000 Beli

Once you have purchased the style, you cannot immediately use all of its moves. You must actively level up your Sword stat by engaging in combat to unlock the style's full potential. 

| Skill Name | Keybind | Required Sword Level | Skill Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cross Slash** | Z | 50 | Launches a sharp, X-shaped air projectile toward the target. |
| **Twister** | X | 150 | Creates a powerful localized whirlwind that knocks back nearby opponents. |

Many players find that V1 is an excellent starting weapon for mid-game grinding. The Cross Slash provides a reliable ranged option to pull enemies from a distance, while the Twister serves as a great defensive tool when you get surrounded by aggressive mobs.

## Ascending to Greatness: How to Get Two Sword Style V2

While the base version of this weapon style is formidable, the real power lies in upgrading to the **haze seas two sword style** V2. This upgraded version significantly boosts your damage output, increases the size of your projectiles, and changes how your skills behave in active combat.

### Defeating the Dual Swordsman Superboss

The bottleneck for most players attempting to unlock the **haze seas two sword style** V2 is the Dual Swordsman Superboss. Community reports indicate that this boss has a high health pool and can easily shred unprepared players with rapid slash combinations. 

The Skill Book required for the upgrade has a **10% drop rate**. This means you will likely need to defeat the Superboss multiple times before the book drops. 

According to player experience, the best strategy for farming this boss is to team up with a group of friends or utilize high-mobility Devil Fruits. Keep your distance, dodge his incoming wind slashes, and punish his recovery animations with heavy ranged attacks. Once the book drops, immediately head back to Doctor White in Logue Town to claim your upgrade.

## Combat Breakdown: V1 vs. V2 Move Showcase

Upgrading your style does not just increase your raw numbers; it fundamentally changes the utility of your moveset. The moveset of the **haze seas two sword style** V2 makes you a much bigger threat in PvP scenarios by turning localized defensive moves into offensive ranged tools.

Here is a direct comparison of how the moves evolve from V1 to V2:

| Move Keybind | V1 Skill Name & Effect | V2 Skill Name & Upgrade | Tactical Advantage in V2 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Z Key** | **Cross Slash:** Launches a standard X-shaped projectile. | **Cross Slash V2:** Launches a massive, high-damage air projectile. | Larger hitbox makes it much harder for opponents to dodge. |
| **X Key** | **Twister:** Creates a localized whirlwind that knocks back nearby targets. | **Twister V2:** Launches a powerful whirlwind directly toward the opponent. | Allows you to crowd-control and stun enemies from a safe distance. |

## Haze Seas Two Sword Style vs. Other Fighting Styles

While the **haze seas two sword style** is a premier choice for sword mains, players often compare it to other V2 combat styles available in the game. Depending on your preferred playstyle, you might find that a martial arts style suits you better, or you may prefer to stick with the dual blades.

Based on recent showcases and community reports, here is how the dual swords stack up against the game's other prominent V2 fighting styles:

### Black Leg V2
This style focuses heavily on fire-based kicks and high mobility. The *Stampede* move allows the user to teleport closer to their target, making it excellent for closing gaps. Its ultimate move, *Azure Inferno*, covers the user's legs in blue flames, boosting damage. Compared to the dual swords, Black Leg V2 offers superior mobility but lacks the consistent ranged zoning of the swords.

### Electro V2
Electro V2 is a fast-paced, spammable fighting style. Moves like *Electrifying Tackle* allow players to drag opponents into the air to start aerial combos. While it offers excellent combo potential, it requires you to get up close and personal, whereas the dual swords allow you to fight safely from a distance.

### Cyborg V2
Cyborg V2 is all about explosive power and visual flair. The signature move, *Radical Beam*, fires a massive energy beam that shakes the screen and deals heavy damage. It is fantastic for PvE grinding, but its long startup animations make it easier to dodge in PvP compared to the swift projectiles of the dual swords.

### Dragon Claw V2
This style is highly valued in team fights due to its crowd-control capabilities. The *Draconic Vortex* move acts like a vacuum, pulling in nearby opponents and stunning them. While Dragon Claw V2 is the king of setups, the dual swords still hold the edge in raw, consistent DPS for solo players.

## Best Builds and Stats for Dual Swordsmen

To maximize the potential of the **haze seas two sword style**, you need to build your character correctly. Because this style scales entirely off your Sword stat, putting points into Melee or Devil Fruit will not help your sword damage.

### Recommended Stat Allocation

Depending on your level cap and playstyle, you should distribute your stat points to complement your dual-wielding capabilities.

*   **Sword Stat (70%):** This should always be your highest stat. Maximizing this ensures that your Cross Slash and Twister deal the highest possible damage.
*   **Defense Stat (20%):** You need enough health to survive boss fights and PvP combos. Do not neglect your defense, especially when farming the Dual Swordsman Superboss.
*   **Stamina/Utility (10%):** Having enough stamina is crucial for dashing and dodging enemy attacks. 

### Fruit Synergies

Many players prefer the **haze seas two sword style** because it pairs incredibly well with utility-focused Devil Fruits. Since you do not need to rely on fruit damage, you can choose a fruit based entirely on its crowd control or mobility.

1.  **Ice Fruit:** The freeze abilities of the Ice Fruit allow you to lock an opponent in place, making it incredibly easy to land a fully charged V2 Twister.
2.  **Sand Fruit:** Sand offers excellent trapping moves that stop enemies in their tracks, setting up devastating sword combos.
3.  **Light or Door Fruit:** These fruits offer unparalleled map mobility, allowing you to quickly travel between islands to farm bosses or escape bad situations in PvP.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I get the haze seas two sword style V2?
To obtain the V2 upgrade, you must first purchase the V1 style from Doctor White in Logue Town for $100k Beli. Next, you must farm the Dual Swordsman Superboss until he drops the Skill Book (which has a 10% drop rate). Finally, bring the book back to Doctor White to unlock the V2 moveset.

### Where is Doctor White located?
Doctor White can be found in Logue City (also known as Logue Town). He is the NPC trainer responsible for teaching both the V1 and V2 variants of the dual sword combat style.

### Is the haze seas two sword style good for grinding?
Yes, it is highly rated for grinding. The V1 version is affordable for mid-game players, and the V2 version's launched Twister projectile allows you to gather and defeat groups of enemies from a safe distance without taking damage.

### What stats should I level up for this style?
You should focus primarily on your Sword stat, as all of the style's damage scales directly with it. Allocate secondary points into Defense for survivability and Stamina for dodging.
