## Understanding the Power of Kenbunshoku Haki

In the world of Haze Seas, which you can play on the [Roblox official platform](https://www.roblox.com), players must balance their offensive output with reliable defensive tools. While Devil Fruits provide raw destructive power, Haki provides the utility needed to survive chaotic battlefields. Having the haze seas observation haki active gives you a distinct tactical advantage by altering how you perceive the battlefield and automatically shielding you from incoming damage.

When you toggle this ability on using the default keybind (R), your character gains heightened senses. This is represented visually by a shift in your screen's presentation, allowing you to track targets through solid structures and over vast distances. 

Here is a breakdown of what this skill offers when active:

*   **X-Ray Vision:** You can see player outlines, names, levels, and health bars through walls and obstacles.
*   **Automatic Dodging:** Your character will automatically phase through incoming attacks, taking zero damage as long as you have dodge charges remaining.
*   **Dodge Counter:** A clear indicator appears above your character's head, showing you exactly how many dodges you have left before the ability enters a cooldown state.

According to community reports, it is important to note that the wall-tracking feature only applies to player characters. You cannot see generic environmental NPCs through walls with this ability, though the automatic dodging mechanic still works perfectly against NPC attacks.

## How to Unlock Level 1 Haze Seas Observation Haki

To obtain the first level of haze seas observation haki, you must sail to the Marine Base Town. This is a high-level zone designed for players who have reached at least Level 600. If you attempt to purchase the skill before reaching this level milestone, the trainer NPC will refuse to teach you.

Once you arrive at Marine Base Town, you need to navigate to the highest point of the island. The trainer NPC stands at the top of the towering marine structures. You will need to use your climbing skills or previous movement upgrades like Sky Walk to reach him.

In addition to the level requirement, you must bring a substantial amount of currency. The trainer charges 150,000 Beli to unlock the base version of the skill. Once purchased, you can immediately toggle the ability on and off using the R key.

For players who rely on Logia-type Devil Fruits, player experience suggests that Observation Haki acts as an extra layer of security. ## Reaching the Next Level at Bubble Island

As you progress into the late-game content, the standard number of dodges provided by the base skill might not be enough to keep you safe. Upgrading your haze seas observation haki to the next tier is highly recommended once you reach the second sea.

The Level 2 trainer is located at Bubble Island, a zone meant for seasoned high-level players. The requirements for this upgrade are significantly steeper, reflecting the power boost it provides. You must be at least Level 1500 and possess 200,000 Beli to purchase the upgrade.

## Synergizing Observation Haki with Other Movement Skills

To become truly untouchable, you should not rely on evasion alone. Combining your defensive Haki with other utility skills creates a highly versatile combat style. Let us look at how the haze seas observation haki stacks up against and works alongside the other major utility skills in the game.

### Sky Walk (Wind Walker)
Unlocked at the Starter Island inside a hidden cave for 20,000 Beli, Sky Walk allows you to jump up to 10 times in mid-air. When combined with Observation Haki, you can hover above danger, spot enemies through the terrain, and safely drop down to strike while keeping your dodge charges ready.

### Flash Step (Swift Step)
Purchased at Clown Island (Level 40+) for 25,000 Beli, Flash Step lets you teleport short distances by pressing the T key. If your dodge charges run low while your Haki is active, you can quickly Flash Step away to let your dodges regenerate.

### Armament Haki (Busoshoku)
Taught by Rayleigh at Loguetown (Level 350+) for players who are at least Level 250. Activated with the J key, this skill coats your weapons and fists, boosting your damage output. While Observation Haki keeps you safe, Armament Haki ensures your counterattacks hit as hard as possible.

## PvP and PvE Evasion Strategies

Using the haze seas observation haki effectively in PvP and PvE requires resource management. You cannot simply turn the ability on and expect to remain invincible forever. If you let multiple enemies hit you simultaneously, your dodge charges will deplete in seconds, leaving you vulnerable while the skill goes on cooldown.

Here are some actionable tips to maximize your evasion efficiency:

*   **Bait and Switch:** In PvP, keep your Haki turned off initially. Wait for your opponent to unleash their high-damage combo, then toggle it on (R) to absorb their strongest moves.
*   **Watch the Indicator:** Always keep an eye on the dodge counter above your head. According to player experiences, if you drop to one dodge left, it is time to use Flash Step or Sky Walk to retreat and let the charges reset.
*   **Logia Conservation:** If you have a Logia fruit, turn off your Haki when fighting weak mobs that cannot hurt you anyway. Save your dodge charges for elite enemies and bosses who use Haki-infused attacks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I unlock the haze seas observation haki?
To unlock the base version of this ability, you must travel to Marine Base Town once you reach Level 600. Locate the trainer NPC standing at the very top of the island's towers and pay him 150,000 Beli.

### Can I use the haze seas observation haki alongside Armament Haki?
Yes, you can have both types of Haki active at the same time. Press J to activate your Armament Haki for offensive buffs, and press R to turn on your Observation Haki for defensive dodging and tracking.

### Where is the Level 2 trainer located?
The Level 2 trainer is located at Bubble Island. You must be at least Level 1500 and have 200,000 Beli to purchase this upgrade, which adds two extra dodge charges to your pool.

### Why can't I see NPCs through walls with my Haki active?
The wall-tracking visual effect is designed to highlight other players (enemies and allies) by showing their names, levels, and health bars. Community reports confirm that this tracking feature does not display standard island NPCs.
