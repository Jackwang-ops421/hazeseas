## The Core Haki Types in Haze Seas

There are three distinct forms of Haki available in the game, each serving a unique tactical purpose in both PvE grinding and PvP encounters. To plan your progression route, it is best to understand what each type offers and how they fit into your character build.

*   **Buso Haki (Armament):** Coats your limbs and weapons in a dark armor. This is your primary tool for boosting raw damage and hitting elemental (Logia) fruit users who are otherwise immune to physical attacks.
*   **Observation Haki (Kenbunshoku):** Grants you heightened sensory awareness, allowing you to auto-dodge a set number of incoming attacks and see enemy health bars clearly.
*   **Conqueror's Haki (Haoshoku):** An elite, passive-active ability that unleashes a powerful shockwave to stun and incapacitate weaker enemies around you.

## How to Get Buso Haki (Armament)

Buso Haki is the first major milestone for physical damage dealers, sword mains, and fruit users alike. Without it, fighting high-level elemental bosses becomes an uphill battle. 

To learn Buso Haki, you must seek out the **Buso Trainer** (commonly known as Rayleigh or Old Man Silver). He can be found standing in **Logue Town** (for Level 1 training) and on **Revolutionary Island** (for Level 2 training).

## How to Get Observation Haki (Kenbunshoku)

If Buso is your ultimate sword, Observation Haki is your ultimate shield. Activating this ability applies a bright outline to all nearby entities, showing their levels and exact HP values. More importantly, it grants you automatic dodges, making you temporarily untouchable during chaotic boss fights.

To unlock this sensory power, travel to the **Observation Haki Trainer**. This NPC can be found in several locations, including **Marine Base**, **Bubble Island**, and **Desert City**.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Marine Base / Bubble Island / Desert City | Level 600 | $150,000 | 3 Dodges | R |
| **Level 2** | Marine Base / Bubble Island / Desert City | Level 1500 | $200,000 | 5 Dodges | R |

### How to Level Up Observation Haki Fast
To train and level up your Observation Haki, you must keep the ability active (by pressing the **R** key) and allow enemies to hit you. Each successful auto-dodge contributes experience toward your Haki mastery. Experienced players recommend gathering a group of fast-attacking, low-level mob NPCs, cornering yourself, and letting them strike you repeatedly to quickly max out your dodge capacity.

## How to Unlock Conqueror's Haki (Haoshoku)

Conqueror's Haki is the rarest and most prestigious power in the game. Unlike the other two forms, you cannot simply walk up to a standard island trainer and buy it with Beli. Instead, it is tied to your character's bloodline, special achievements, or premium store purchases.

When activated, Conqueror's Haki unleashes a massive red and black spiritual pressure wave. This wave instantly stuns or knocks out weaker, non-boss NPC enemies in your immediate vicinity. Furthermore, if two players activate their Conqueror's Haki near each other, it triggers a dramatic, screen-shaking "Haki Clash" visual effect.

## Essential Movement Abilities to Pair with Haki

To make the most of your spiritual powers, you need high mobility. In the world of One Piece games, positioning is everything. You should pair your combat aura with these two essential mobility techniques:

### Flash Step (Soru)
Flash Step allows you to teleport instantly to your cursor's location within a set radius. It is perfect for closing the distance to unleash a Buso-enhanced physical strike or escaping a boss's area-of-effect attack. You can learn this from the Flash Step Trainer on **Clown Island** (Level 1), **Three Islands** (Level 2), or **Bubble Island** (Level 3).

### Sky Walk (Geppo)
Sky Walk allows you to jump repeatedly through the air, bypassing difficult terrain and staying out of reach of ground-bound enemies. To find the trainer, head to the tall stone pillars near the Starter Island. Look for a hidden cave entrance on one of the pillars, climb to the top, and speak with the Blackleg NPC.

2. **Double-Check Your Stats Before Resetting:** If you are an older player returning to the game, your stats, fruits, and fighting styles should still be intact. Do not click the reset button in the Level 1 Starter Island tavern unless you want a completely fresh, permanent restart.

4. **Utilize Codes and Daily Rewards:** Check the developers' official social channels or the gift icon in the in-game menu to redeem active promotional codes. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What level do I need to be to get Haki in Haze Seas?
The level requirement depends on the specific type of Haki you want to learn. You can get Level 1 Buso (Armament) Haki at Level 350, while Level 1 Observation Haki requires you to be Level 600. Conqueror's Haki has no level requirement if you obtain it via the D Clan race or the Game Pass, though you will need at least 250 stamina to activate it.

### Why can't I damage elemental users even with my sword?
To damage players or NPCs using Elemental (Logia) Devil Fruits, you must activate your **haze seas haki**—specifically Buso (Armament) Haki—by pressing the **J** key. This coats your weapons in a black aura, allowing you to bypass their natural physical immunity.

### Where is the Buso Haki trainer located?
The Level 1 Buso Trainer (Rayleigh) is located in Logue Town (Level 350 area). The Level 2 Buso Trainer can be found on Revolutionary Island, which requires your character to be at least Level 1150.

### How do I upgrade my Observation Haki to Level 2?
First, ensure you have reached character Level 1500. Then, gather $200,000 Beli and visit the Observation Haki Trainer at Marine Base, Bubble Island, or Desert City to purchase the Level 2 upgrade, which increases your maximum auto-dodges from 3 to 5.
