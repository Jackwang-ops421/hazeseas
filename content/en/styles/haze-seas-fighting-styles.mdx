## Base Fighting Styles: Locations and How to Obtain

To progress through the First and Second Seas, you will need to move past your default fists. Let's take a look at the base **haze seas fighting styles** currently available to players and how you can add them to your arsenal.

### Combat
This is the default fighting style that every player automatically receives upon joining the game for the first time. It features a basic punch combo and serves as a placeholder until you can afford a specialized style. If you ever want to return to Combat after learning another style, you can talk to the NPC teacher of your current style to switch back for free.

### Black Leg
Inspired by legendary kick-based martial arts, Black Leg is the go-to style for early-game players. It is highly cost-effective at only 50,000 Beli and can be purchased from the Blackleg Teacher at the Sea Restaurant in the First Sea. This style is characterized by fiery kicks, rapid multi-hit strikes, and excellent area-of-effect (AoE) damage that helps clear groups of grinding mobs quickly.

### Electro Style
If you have reached the Second Sea and accumulated some wealth, the Electro Style is a massive upgrade. Sold by the Bunny Girl NPC inside Udon Prison for 450,000 Beli, this style utilizes electrical energy to stun and blitz opponents. It offers faster M1 attack speeds and highly disruptive lightning-based skills that make it a favorite for PvP enthusiasts.

## Detailed Move Analysis for V2 Fighting Styles

To help you decide which upgrade path to prioritize, let's look at the specific movesets for each V2 fighting style based on early gameplay showcases.

### Black Leg V2 (Awakened Kicks)
This style upgrades your standard orange flames to a brilliant, high-temperature blue flame aesthetic. It is designed to keep you glued to your target.
*   **Demonic Crash (Z Move):** The player leaps into the air and crashes down violently on the target area, crushing defenses and dealing heavy AoE damage.
*   **Tempest Whirlwind (X Move):** A spinning kick that creates a vortex of blue fire, dragging nearby enemies into the hitbox.
*   **Stampede (C Move):** A highly useful utility move that teleports the player directly in front of the opponent or toward the cursor, allowing for instant follow-ups.
*   **Azure Inferno (V Move):** The ultimate move that covers your legs in intense blue fire, significantly boosting your damage output and changing your attack visual effects.

### Electro V2 (Awakened Lightning)
Electro V2 focuses on speed, aerial superiority, and keeping your opponent locked in hitstun.
*   **Electrifying Tackle (Z Move):** A forward dash that grabs the opponent, drags them up into the air, and slams them back down. It serves as an excellent combo starter.
*   **Electro Thunder Collapse (X Move):** The player calls down a massive bolt of lightning, destroying the ground around them.
*   **Lightning Beast Barrage (C Move):** A highly spammable projectile attack that releases multiple lightning wolves to track and pressure fleeing targets.

### Fishman V2 (Awakened Water Combat)
Fishman V2 trades close-range speed for incredible range and zoning capabilities, making it feel almost like a hybrid fruit style.
*   **Hydra Launch (Z Move):** Launches a fast-moving water projectile that pushes enemies back.
*   **Poseidon Trident (X Move):** Fires a long-range trident made of condensed water and electricity, allowing you to poke opponents from a safe distance.
*   **Tidal Descent (C Move):** A heavy slam that sends shockwaves through the ground, punishing enemies who get too close.

### Cyborg V2 (Mechanical Might)
If you enjoy heavy impact sounds, screen shake, and explosive damage, Cyborg V2 is the premier choice.
*   **Radical Smash (Z Move):** A heavy, mechanical fist slam that breaks blocks and leaves a crater.
*   **Radical Beam (X Move):** A massive energy beam fired from the hands, reminiscent of classic anime energy blasts, that melts health bars at long range.
*   **Radical Barrage (C Move):** A rapid-fire punch combo that locks the target in place before delivering a final explosive punch.

### Dragon Claw V2 (Dragon Master)
Dragon Claw V2 is built around controlling the battlefield and setting up team combos with its powerful crowd control.
*   **Draconic Kick (Z Move):** A wide-angle sweep that clears out enemies in front of you.
*   **Draconic Flames (X Move):** A continuous breath of dragon fire that acts as a mid-range beam.
*   **Draconic Vortex (C Move):** A powerful special move that charges up and creates a vacuum vortex. It pulls all nearby enemies into the center and stuns them, leaving them completely vulnerable to follow-up attacks.

## Combat Tips and Synergy Strategies

Choosing from the pool of **haze seas fighting styles** is only half the battle. To maximize your effectiveness in PvP and PvE, you must learn how to build around your chosen style.

### Synergizing with Devil Fruits
Because fighting styles like Dragon Claw V2 and Electro V2 offer excellent stun and pull mechanics, they pair perfectly with high-damage, slow-startup Devil Fruits. For example, using *Draconic Vortex* to group and stun enemies allows you to easily land massive, slow-casting fruit ultimates without fear of the enemy dodging.

### Managing Elemental Intangibility
Keep in mind that using standard fighting style attacks on players or NPCs with active elemental intangibility (often referred to as Logia dodge) will result in zero damage. To bypass this defense, you must active your Haki or use specific elemental moves built into styles like Black Leg's *Azure Inferno*.

### Range and Positioning
While swords generally outrange fighting styles, V2 styles close this gap. When fighting a sword user, use mobility tools like Black Leg V2's *Stampede* to teleport past their zoning attacks, or use Cyborg V2's *Radical Beam* to force them to play defensively from a distance.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### How do I switch between different haze seas fighting styles?
To switch your style, simply travel to the NPC teacher of the style you want to equip and talk to them. If you have already purchased the style previously, relearning it is entirely free. You can also revert to the default Combat style by talking to your current style's teacher.

### What is the total cost to obtain all base fighting styles?
To unlock all base styles, you will need a total of 500,000 Beli (50,000 Beli for Black Leg and 450,000 Beli for Electro Style). The starting Combat style is free.

### Do fighting styles work against elemental intangibility?
No, standard physical attacks from fighting styles will not damage players or NPCs who have elemental intangibility active. You will need to utilize specialized moves (like fiery kick buffs) or activate Haki to bypass this defense.
