## How to Obtain the Electro Fighting Style

To begin your journey with the **haze seas electro** style, you must navigate to the Second Sea. Specifically, you are looking for the Udon Prison location. Unlike the Black Leg style, which is found relatively early at the Sea Restaurant, Electro requires a bit more investment and exploration.

The trainer for this style is the Bunny Girl NPC. Players often report that finding her is the first real hurdle, as she is tucked away in the Udon Prison area. Once located, you will need to meet the financial requirement to learn the art of the Minks. While older versions of the game or different server iterations might show varying costs, the standard rate in the Second Sea is approximately 450,000 Beli.

The real power of this style is unlocked through the V2 evolution. ## Detailed Move Set Showcase

The **haze seas electro** style is built for players who enjoy a "blitz" playstyle. It focuses on closing the gap between you and your opponent instantly and then following up with multi-hit barrages that leave the enemy stunned.

### Electro V1 Moves
1.  **Electric Tackle:** A straightforward dash that coats the user in electricity, dealing damage to anyone in the path.
2.  **Mink Discharge:** An AoE (Area of Effect) burst that knocks back nearby enemies.
3.  **Electric Slam:** A vertical strike that brings the hammer down on opponents, perfect for ending a ground combo.

### Electro V2 Moves (The Awakening)
The V2 version replaces these with more "polishing" and powerful variants. Community feedback highlights that while these moves are visually stunning, they can occasionally suffer from slight delays in high-ping environments, so timing is everything.

*   **Electrifying Tackle:** An upgraded dash with a much larger hitbox and faster travel speed. The yellow electricity trails behind the player, creating a lingering damage zone.
*   **Electro Thunder Clap:** A massive clap that releases a shockwave. It is excellent for clearing mobs or catching a dodging player.
*   **Lightning Beast Barrage:** The crown jewel of the kit. The user summons a spectral beast made of lightning to strike the opponent repeatedly. This move can often be held down to extend the duration of the attack, allowing for massive damage if the opponent's dodge is on cooldown.

## Pro Tips for Mastery and Farming

Mastering the **haze seas electro** style requires more than just unlocking the moves; you need to understand the rhythm of the game's combat. Since fighting styles are often used in conjunction with Fruits, you should look for Fruits that provide stuns or "stiff" effects to set up your Lightning Beast Barrage.

1.  **Gems are Priority:** Since V2 requires 500 Gems, start saving early. Do not spend Gems on low-tier Fruit spins if you intend to main a fighting style.
2.  **Raging Core Farming:** Raging Cores are rare. Coordinate with a crew to take down world bosses in the Second Sea, as these are the most reliable sources for the evolution materials.
3.  **Haki Synergy:** Always ensure your Busoshoku Haki is active during the Lightning Beast Barrage. Because this move hits multiple times, the damage bonus from Haki scales exponentially.
4.  **The "Hold" Mechanic:** Some V2 moves, like those in the Cyborg and Electro kits, allow you to hold the key down for a prolonged effect. Practice the timing in the training area to maximize your "Radical Beam" or "Lightning Beast" uptime.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where can I find the trainer for the haze seas electro style?
The trainer, known as the Bunny Girl, is located in the Second Sea at Udon Prison. You will need 450,000 Beli to purchase the base V1 version of the style. Once you have the style, you can later visit the Sage of Combat to begin the V2 evolution process.

### What are the specific requirements for Electro V2?
To upgrade your **haze seas electro** to V2, you must collect 2 Raging Cores and 500 Gems. You then need to speak with the Sage of Combat NPC. This evolution changes your lightning color from blue/white to yellow and significantly increases your damage and move set efficiency.

### Is Electro better than Black Leg or Fish-Man Karate?
It depends on your playstyle. **haze seas electro** is superior for mobility and rapid-fire stuns, making it excellent for catching fast opponents. Black Leg (V2) is often preferred for raw burn damage, while Fish-Man Karate offers better knockback and "Hydro" moves that can be useful for controlling the battlefield.

### How do I get Raging Cores for the evolution?
Raging Cores are typically obtained as rare drops from powerful bosses in the Second Sea. Players often participate in "Sea Beast" raids or high-level boss rotations to farm these items. Because they are rare, it is recommended to join a group or faction to increase your farming efficiency.
