## The Power of the Black Leg Style in Haze Piece

In the vast world of Roblox's Haze Piece, choosing the right combat kit is the difference between ruling the waves and sinking to the bottom. If you are looking for a high-mobility, fire-infused combat kit, the **haze seas black leg** fighting style is an essential pick for mid-game players. This guide will walk you through every step of acquiring the **haze seas black leg** and mastering its powerful kicks to dominate your enemies.

Fighting styles serve as the backbone of your character's utility, especially when you are waiting for a high-tier Devil Fruit or simply prefer the aesthetic of martial arts. The Black Leg style, inspired by the legendary chef Sanji, offers a unique blend of area-of-effect (AoE) attacks and ranged pressure. Beyond the damage, it provides a mechanical advantage that many other styles lack: the ability to cancel stuns. In a fast-paced RPG environment where one stun can lead to a quick defeat, this feature makes the style a top-tier choice for both PvE farming and early PvP encounters.

## How to Unlock the Haze Seas Black Leg Style

Acquiring this fighting style requires a bit of preparation. Unlike starter styles that might be available on the first island, you will need to prove your strength and save up a decent amount of currency. To find the trainer, you must navigate to the Sea Restaurant, a recognizable landmark for any fan of the genre.

According to community reports and in-game data, the trainer for this style is the NPC **Sanji**. He is stationed at the Sea Restaurant, specifically located to the left of the NPC Naomi. However, simply finding him isn't enough; you must meet specific level and financial requirements before he will agree to teach you his signature moves.

### Requirements at a Glance

| Requirement Type | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **NPC Trainer** | Sanji |
| **Location** | Sea Restaurant |
| **Minimum Level** | Level 250 |
| **Cost** | 50,000 Beli |
| **Special Attribute** | Stun Cancellation |

Reaching level 250 is the first major hurdle. Players typically reach this milestone by completing quests on the starter islands and moving through the progression path of the First Sea. Once you hit the level cap and have 50,000 Beli in your pocket, head to the Sea Restaurant to make your purchase. 

## Detailed Skill Breakdown and Mastery

Once you have purchased the **haze seas black leg** style, your journey is only beginning. You will start with basic melee attacks, but the true power of the style is locked behind Mastery points. Mastery is earned by using the style in combat, defeating NPCs, and completing bosses. As you increase your Mastery level, you will unlock four distinct skills that define the Black Leg's kit.

### Skill Progression Table

| Skill Key | Skill Name | Mastery Required | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Z** | Ground Smash | 30 | The user curls into a ball, launches into the air, and slams down to create a shockwave. |
| **X** | Party Table | 60 | A multi-hit spinning attack performed on the head, hitting all nearby enemies. |
| **C** | Air Barrage | 90 | A series of rapid kicks that fire air-burst projectiles toward the mouse cursor. |
| **V** | Diablo Mode | 120 | A transformation that adds flame visuals and significantly buffs the damage of all moves. |

### Analyzing the Moveset

The **Ground Smash (Z)** is your primary engagement tool. Because it involves a leap, it can be used to dodge ground-based attacks while simultaneously repositioning yourself for a counter-attack. Player experience suggests that this move is excellent for clearing groups of low-level mobs that have gathered around you.

**Party Table (X)** is the quintessential AoE (Area of Effect) move. By spinning on your head, you create a "dead zone" for enemies. If you are surrounded by NPCs during a farming session, activating Party Table can hit multiple targets several times, maximizing your damage output per second (DPS).

**Air Barrage (C)** provides the style with much-needed versatility. While Black Leg is primarily a melee style, Air Barrage allows you to stay at a distance. Since the bursts follow your mouse pointer, it requires a bit of aim, but it is incredibly effective for kitting bosses or finishing off runners in PvP.

Finally, **Diablo Mode (V)** is the crown jewel of the **haze seas black leg** kit. While the initial activation spin doesn't deal damage, the subsequent buff is massive. Every kick and skill used while Diablo Mode is active gains a fire trail and increased damage. It transforms a reliable style into a powerhouse capable of melting through high-health targets.

## Combat Strategy and Stun Canceling

One of the most significant hidden benefits of the **haze seas black leg** style is its ability to cancel stuns. In [Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/) combat games, "stun-locking" is a common tactic where an opponent hits you with a move that prevents you from moving or attacking. Black Leg's unique properties allow players to break out of these animations more reliably than other styles.

### Tactical Tips for Success:
*   **Keep Diablo Mode Active:** Always prioritize reaching 120 Mastery. The damage difference between base form and Diablo Mode is substantial. In long boss fights, ensure you reactivate it as soon as the cooldown permits.
*   **The "Z to X" Combo:** A common strategy among veteran players is to use Ground Smash to get into the center of a pack of enemies, followed immediately by Party Table. This ensures that you are hitting the maximum number of targets with your highest-hitting AoE.
*   **Mouse Tracking:** Practice your aim with Air Barrage. Because it follows the cursor, you can "lead" your shots against moving targets, making it a viable tool for catching players who are trying to fly or dash away.
*   **Stun Baiting:** Since you know you have better stun recovery, you can afford to play slightly more aggressively. You can often bait an opponent into using their stun move, cancel it, and then punish them while their skills are on cooldown.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where exactly is the trainer for the haze seas black leg style?
The trainer, Sanji, is located at the Sea Restaurant. Once you arrive at the restaurant, look for the NPC named Naomi; Sanji is located just to the left of her.

### Does Diablo Mode increase the damage of my Devil Fruit?
No, Diablo Mode specifically buffs the moves associated with the **haze seas black leg** fighting style and your basic melee M1 attacks. It does not provide a damage boost to your Fruit abilities or Sword skills.

### What is the best way to level up Mastery for Black Leg?
The most efficient way to level Mastery is to use the style to land the finishing blow on high-level NPCs or bosses. Many players recommend farming the bosses at the Sea Restaurant or Marine Base, as they provide a good balance of XP and Mastery points.

### Can I switch to another fighting style after buying Black Leg?
Yes, you can switch fighting styles by visiting other trainers. However, you will need to pay the fee for the new style. The good news is that your Mastery progress for the **haze seas black leg** is usually saved, so if you decide to switch back later, you won't have to start from zero.
