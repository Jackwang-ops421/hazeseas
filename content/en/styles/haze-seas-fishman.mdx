## What is the Haze Seas Fishman Fighting Style?

In the vast oceans of this Roblox RPG, combat styles define your character's utility and damage output. The Fishman style, inspired by the legendary Fishman Karate from the One Piece universe, utilizes the moisture in the air and the surrounding sea to deliver devastating shockwaves. Unlike standard melee attacks, this style focuses on fluid, wave-like movements that make your attack patterns highly unpredictable.

To fully understand this style, players must venture to Fishman Island, a vibrant yet dangerous underwater paradise. This location serves as the hub for the Fishman tribe and contains valuable quests that help mid-game players level up efficiently. According to community reports, players will encounter the formidable Neptune King here, a boss known for his precise trident techniques.

If you are planning to farm on Fishman Island or seek out the style's secrets, you should familiarize yourself with the local quest line. Coral Grimes is the primary quest giver on the island, offering tasks tailored for players between levels 1600 and 1700.

### Fishman Island Quest Line

| Quest Giver | Required Level | Objective | Rewards & Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Coral Grimes | Level 1600 | Defeat 5 Island Fishmen | Leveling and Beli farming |
| Coral Grimes | Level 1650 | Defeat 4 Fishman Elites | Advanced leveling |
| Coral Grimes | Level 1700 | Defeat 1 Neptune King | Chance to obtain the Neptune Trident |

Farming these quests is an excellent way to accumulate the currency needed to purchase new fighting styles. Additionally, defeating the Neptune King gives players a chance to obtain his legendary trident from a mysterious merchant on the island, which pairs perfectly with water-based builds.

## How to Unlock Fishman Karate V1

Before you can access the high-tier V2 moves, you must first purchase the basic V1 version of the style. The Fishman Trainer is located in the central trainer hub alongside the instructors for Black Leg, Electro, Cyborg, and Dragon Claw. 

To purchase the V1 style, you will need 50,000 Beli (or the equivalent basic currency). This is a relatively low cost, making it highly accessible for players transitioning from the early game to the mid-game. Once purchased, you will unlock a three-move set that forms the foundation of your water-based combat.

### Fishman V1 Moveset Overview

| Move Name | Description | Combat Utility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Shark Fist | A powerful, straight-line punch wrapped in water energy. | Great for single-target damage and knockback. |
| Shark Barrage | A rapid succession of water-infused punches. | Ideal for breaking enemy shields and dealing sustained damage. |
| Titanic Slam | A heavy ground slam that releases a shockwave of water. | Excellent for clearing groups of minor mobs (AoE). |

While V1 is decent for early farming, player experience suggests that it lacks the speed and combo potential required for high-level PvP. To truly compete, you will need to seek out the Sage of Combat and begin the awakening process.

## How to Awaken the Haze Seas Fishman V2 Style

To upgrade your martial arts, you must find the Sage of Combat NPC. When you transition to the upgraded **haze seas fishman** style, the Sage of Combat will require you to prove your worth by gathering rare materials and paying a significant fee. 

For the **haze seas fishman** V2, players report needing two Sea Beast Hearts and 500,000 Beli (or Gems, depending on the current game version). Sea Beast Hearts are rare drops obtained by defeating Sea Beasts that spawn randomly in the open ocean. Defeating these colossal monsters requires a sturdy ship and group coordination, making the V2 upgrade a true badge of honor.

### Fishman V2 Upgrade Requirements

| Required Material / Currency | Quantity | Acquisition Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Sea Beast Hearts | 2 | Dropped by Sea Beasts in the open ocean |
| Beli / Gems | 500,000 | Quest completion and boss farming |
| Base Style | V1 Fishman | Purchased from the Fishman Trainer |

Once the evolution is complete, your moveset will transform. The V2 version introduces highly polished visual animations, including glowing water effects and trident attacks. The two primary moves showcased by the community include:

*   **Hydro Launch:** A rapid blitz move where the player dashes straight through the enemy, dragging them along in a swirling vortex of water and finishing with a trident strike.
*   **Idle Descent:** A clean, aerial drop attack that crashes down on opponents, creating a massive water explosion that deals heavy area-of-effect damage.

These moves offer much higher mobility than the V1 set, allowing you to close distances instantly and lock opponents into long stuns.

## Best Combos and Build Strategy

To maximize the damage of the **haze seas fishman** skills, you must optimize your character stats and equipment. Because fighting styles scale directly with your Melee stat, you should dump a significant portion of your stat points into Melee.

Furthermore, integrating Haki is essential. Utilizing Armament Haki (Busoshoku) will apply a massive damage multiplier to all your water strikes. Observation Haki (Kenbunshoku) is also highly recommended, as it allows you to dodge incoming attacks while you execute the slightly longer animations of Hydro Launch.

If you want to play like a true Neptune warrior, consider using a hybrid build. By splitting your stats between Melee and Sword, you can utilize the legendary Neptune Trident alongside your Fishman Karate. Start your combo with the Trident's pull or stun moves, follow up immediately with Hydro Launch to drag the enemy across the arena, and finish with Idle Descent to crush them into the ground.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the trainer for the haze seas fishman style located?
The Fishman Trainer is located in the central starter area alongside the trainers for the other four primary fighting styles (Black Leg, Electro, Cyborg, and Dragon Claw). You can purchase the base V1 style from him for 50,000 Beli.

### What materials are required to awaken Fishman V2?
To awaken the V2 version, you must speak to the Sage of Combat. You will need to provide two Sea Beast Hearts (dropped by Sea Beasts in the ocean) and 500,000 Beli or Gems.

### Is the Fishman fighting style good for PvP?
Yes, the V2 Fishman style is highly regarded in PvP due to the Hydro Launch move, which allows you to blitz through opponents, drag them into combos, and disrupt their positioning. It offers excellent synergy with sword builds.

### How do I get the Neptune Trident?
The Neptune Trident is a rare weapon associated with Fishman Island. You must defeat the Neptune King boss (Level 1700 quest) and speak with the mysterious merchant on the island to obtain it.
