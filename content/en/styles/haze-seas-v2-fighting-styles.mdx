## Understanding Fighting Style Evolution

To begin your journey toward awakening your combat potential, you must first reach the Second Sea. This is where the core progression of the game shifts, allowing you to transition from standard V1 martial arts to their highly destructive V2 counterparts. The central figure in this progression is the Sage of Combat, a mysterious NPC who acts as the gatekeeper to advanced martial arts.

Before you can speak to the Sage of Combat to evolve any style, you must meet a universal mastery threshold. Every single V1 fighting style must be leveled up to at least 300 Mastery. Once you hit this milestone, you will need to gather specialized boss drops and a substantial amount of currency—either Beli (Belly) or Gems—to finalize the evolution.

## Comprehensive Breakdown of All V2 Fighting Styles

Let's dive deep into the specific requirements, costs, and moves for each of the five upgraded styles. Upgrading to the **haze seas v2 fighting styles** is a serious investment, so knowing what each style offers will help you prioritize which one to grind for first.

### Black Leg V2 (Leg of Fury)

Based on the legendary kicks of Sanji, Black Leg V2 turns your legs into blazing columns of azure fire. It is widely considered one of the best mobility and crowd-control styles in the game.

*   **V1 Cost:** 50,000 Beli (from Dark Step Teacher at Sea Restaurant)
*   **V2 Upgrade Cost:** 5,000,000 Beli
*   **Required Materials:** 2x Phantom Lanterns (obtained from the Ghost Ship)
*   **Key Moves:** Demonic Crash, Tempest Whirlwind, Stampede, and Azure Inferno.
*   **Title Unlocked:** Leg of Fury

### Electro V2 (Thunder Lord)

Harnessing the electrical energy of the Mink Tribe, Electro V2 provides incredible speed and rapid-fire stun attacks. It is a favorite among PvP enthusiasts due to its combo-extending capabilities.

*   **V1 Cost:** 450,000 Beli (from Bunny Girl at Udon Prison)
*   **V2 Upgrade Cost:** 500 Gems
*   **Required Materials:** 2x Raizen Cores (obtained from Enel)
*   **Key Moves:** Electro Flying Tackle, Electro Thunderclap, and Lightning Beast Brush.
*   **Title Unlocked:** Thunder Lord

### Cyborg V2 (Steel Titan)

Cyborg V2 is a mechanical marvel, offering heavy-hitting explosive attacks and excellent defensive utility. It is highly favored by players who prefer high-damage area-of-effect (AoE) skills.

*   **V1 Cost:** 800,000 Beli (from Cyborg Specialist)
*   **V2 Upgrade Cost:** 5,000,000 Beli
*   **Required Materials:** 2x Saturn Rings (obtained from the Saturn boss)
*   **Key Moves:** Radical Smash, Radical Beam, and Radical Barrage.
*   **Title Unlocked:** Steel Titan

### Fishman V2 (Tidal Striker)

Using the power of water manipulation, Fishman V2 allows you to control the battlefield with massive shockwaves and fluid strikes. It is particularly effective for sea-based combat and farming large groups of enemies.

*   **V1 Cost:** 450,000 Beli (from Fishman Trainer)
*   **V2 Upgrade Cost:** 5,000,000 Beli
*   **Required Materials:** 2x Sea Beast Hearts (obtained from Sea Beasts)
*   **Title Unlocked:** Tidal Striker

### Dragon Claw V2 (Draconic Master)

Dragon Claw V2 is the pinnacle of raw offensive power. It unleashes fiery draconic energy that melts enemy health bars, making it an exceptional choice for boss raids.

*   **V1 Cost:** 150 Gems (from Dragon Master)
*   **V2 Upgrade Cost:** 500 Gems
*   **Required Materials:** 2x Dragon Hearts (obtained from the Dragon Hybrid boss)
*   **Key Moves:** Draconic Kick, Draconic Flames, and Draconic Vortex.
*   **Title Unlocked:** Draconic Master

## Boss Farming Guide for V2 Materials

Farming the necessary upgrade items is the most time-consuming part of obtaining the **haze seas v2 fighting styles**. Fortunately, most of these bosses have a 100% drop rate for their respective V2 materials, meaning you only need to defeat them twice. The sole exception is the Sea Beast, which requires some patience.

Here is a detailed breakdown of where to find each boss and how to secure their drops:

*   **Enel (Raizen Cores):** Located in the Sky Island region. He is a relatively straightforward boss, but his lightning attacks can stun you if you are not careful. Defeat him twice to guarantee your two Raizen Cores.
*   **Saturn (Saturn Rings):** Found in the Second Sea. Saturn possesses high health and heavy AoE ground-slams. Keep your distance or use high-mobility styles like Black Leg V1 to dodge his attacks.
*   **Dragon Hybrid (Dragon Hearts):** This boss is a formidable opponent with high fire damage. Ensure you have a reliable defensive fruit or high health before engaging. Defeating him guarantees a Dragon Heart.
*   **Ghost Ship (Phantom Lanterns):** Ghost Ships spawn periodically in the open ocean. You must sail out, trigger the encounter, and defeat the ghostly crew to secure the Phantom Lanterns.
*   **Sea Beasts (Sea Beast Hearts):** Sea Beasts spawn randomly in the waters of the Second and Third Seas. Unlike the other bosses, Sea Beast Hearts only have a 25% drop rate. Player experience suggests grouping up with friends to speed up the spawning and killing process.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### How do I upgrade to the **haze seas v2 fighting styles**?
To upgrade to any V2 fighting style, you must first reach 300 Mastery on the corresponding V1 style. Once this is achieved, travel to the Second Sea and locate the Sage of Combat NPC. You will need to present him with two specific boss-drop materials and pay either 5,000,000 Beli or 500 Gems, depending on the style you wish to evolve.

#### Where can I find the Sage of Combat?
The Sage of Combat is located in the central hub of the Second Sea. He stands near the standard fighting style trainers, making it easy to purchase your V1 styles, grind their mastery, and immediately speak to him for the V2 evolution.

#### What is the hardest V2 material to farm?
Most players agree that the Sea Beast Heart (required for Fishman V2) is the hardest material to farm. While other bosses like Enel, Saturn, and the Dragon Hybrid have a guaranteed 100% drop rate for their respective materials, Sea Beasts only have a 25% chance to drop a Heart, requiring multiple spawns and kills.

#### Can I switch between V2 fighting styles for free?
Yes! Once you have paid the initial evolution cost and unlocked a V2 style, you can switch back to it at any time for free. Simply speak to the corresponding style trainer or the Sage of Combat to re-equip your unlocked style without paying the Beli, Gems, or materials again.
