## What is Three Sword Style V2 (3SS V2)?

Three Sword Style V2 is the upgraded progression of the base Three Sword Style (3SS). While the original style is acquired in Sea 2 and features three basic moves, the V2 variant is an endgame powerhouse found exclusively in Sea 3. 

According to player experience and community reports, the V2 style offers significantly higher base damage, faster attack animations, and removes annoying cutscenes that slow down your active grinding loops. It stands toe-to-toe with premium weapons like Dark Blade V2, making it a must-have for free-to-play and competitive players alike.

## Prerequisites to Unlock Haze Seas 3SS V2

Before you can even think about forging the **haze seas 3ss v2**, you must complete a strict checklist of prerequisites. If you do not meet these conditions, the Forge NPC in Sea 3 will simply turn you away.

### 1. Reach Sea 3 (Level 4,200+)
To enter Sea 3, you must be at least Level 4,200. Speak to the Gatekeeper NPC in Sea 2. You will be forced to fight the Red Empress boss (inspired by Shanks). This boss features a challenging two-phase fight with 300,000 HP. Once defeated, she will grant you passage to Sea 3.

### 2. Max Out Base 3SS Mastery
You must equip your original Three Sword Style and grind its mastery level to **Level 300**. 

### 3. Own the Core Legendary Swords
To prove your worth as a master swordsman, you must have owned the three legendary swords that make up the style's lore. These can be displayed or claimed at the sword rock in Sea 3:
*   **Enma:** Obtained from Sea 2 activities.
*   **Shusui (Sue):** Obtained from the Sea 2 castle boss (10% drop rate).
*   **Zenith:** A 100% guaranteed drop from the Zenith Boss on Shadow Island (Sea 2) during the Storm event.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Crafting 3SS V2

Once you have arrived at Sea 3 with your Level 300 3SS V1, your journey to craft the **haze seas 3ss v2** truly begins. The forging process requires rare materials that are both highly expensive and heavily time-gated.

### The Forging Recipe
Go to **The Forge** island in Sea 3 and locate the Forge Master NPC. He will require the following materials to craft the weapon:

| Required Material | Quantity | How to Obtain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ancient Blade Relic** | 5 | Purchased from the Wandering Merchant or crafted |
| **Blade Fragment** | 25 | 10% drop from Sea 3 Sea Beasts, or bought from Merchant |
| **Beli (In-game Cash)** | 4,000,000+ | Farming Sea 3 quests and bosses |
| **3SS V1 Mastery** | Level 300 | Active grinding or overnight AFK training |

```
[3SS V1 Level 300] + [5 Ancient Blade Relics] ---> Speak to Forge Master ---> Unlocks 3SS V2
```

### How to Get Ancient Blade Relics and Blade Fragments
Getting your hands on 5 Ancient Blade Relics is the most time-consuming part of the process. Each Ancient Blade Relic requires **5 Blade Fragments** to craft, meaning you need a total of 25 Blade Fragments if you plan to craft them all manually. 

Alternatively, you can buy both directly from the Wandering Merchant when he spawns.

## Finding the Wandering Merchant: Spawn Locations

The Wandering Merchant is a rare NPC who spawns in Sea 3. He appears randomly once every hour and only stays in the game for **7 minutes** before disappearing. Keeping track of him is essential to gathering your crafting components efficiently.

### Wandering Merchant Inventory & Costs
When you find him, you can buy one of each item per spawn. If you want to speed up the process, players often recommend server hopping to reset his stock.

*   **Ancient Blade Relic:** 100 Gems / 1,000,000 Beli
*   **Blade Fragment:** 50 Gems / 500,000 Beli

### Spawn Locations Table
The merchant has three known spawn points in Sea 3. Make sure to use a high-speed mount or flying item (like a broom or flight-enabled Devil Fruit) to check these spots quickly when the spawn window opens:

| Location ID | Area Description | Visual Landmark |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Location 1** | Near the Giant Cube | Located right next to the geometric cube landmark on the main island. |
| **Location 2** | The Throne Room | High up on the mountain, right next to the logs near the World Government throne castle. |
| **Location 3** | The Wooden Docks | At the very end of the long, structurally unsound wooden dock extending into the ocean. |

## FAQ: Haze Seas 3SS V2

### How do I get the base Three Sword Style (3SS V1)?
You must travel to the Snowy Graveyard in the Second Sea. The base style has a 1% drop rate from the Zoro Boss. Once obtained, you must unlock its skills by reaching Level 200 mastery before upgrading to V2.

### Is haze seas 3ss v2 better than the Dark Blade V2?
While Dark Blade V2 deals incredible single-target damage and is highly favored for PvP, the **haze seas 3ss v2** is widely considered superior for PvE grinding. Its wide area-of-effect slashes, lack of disruptive cutscenes, and swift teleportation attacks allow you to clear groups of enemies much faster.

### Can I get Blade Fragments outside of Sea 3?
No. Blade Fragments are exclusive to Sea 3. You can obtain them either by purchasing them from the Wandering Merchant or as a 10% drop from defeating Sea Beasts that spawn in the waters of Sea 3.

### Does the Wandering Merchant spawn in private servers?
Yes, the Wandering Merchant spawns in private servers on the same hourly schedule. However, server hopping in public servers is often faster if you are actively hunting for relics and fragments.
