## Getting Started: Your First Steps and Rolling for Fruits

When you first spawn into the starter island, your immediate priority should be securing a decent Devil Fruit. While some players resort to begging in the chat, the community strongly advises against this. The game is highly generous with active promo codes that provide free spins and experience boosts. Always redeem these codes first to spin for a fruit yourself.

### Fruit Tiers for Early Grinding

Your early-game goal is to obtain a fruit with strong Area of Effect (AoE) moves. This allows you to gather groups of enemies together and defeat them simultaneously without taking too much damage.

| Fruit | Rarity / Category | Grinding Utility | Why It Is Good for Beginners |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phoenix** | Mythical / Beast | S-Tier | Highly recommended by veteran players for its incredible self-healing and solid AoE attacks. |
| **Ice** | Rare / Elemental | A-Tier | Provides elemental immunity (Logia) against lower-level physical mobs, plus great freeze CC. |
| **Sand** | Rare / Elemental | B-Tier | Excellent crowd control and multi-target damage scaling. |
| **Gum** | Uncommon / Natural | B-Tier (Potential S-Tier) | Decent early on, but requires a massive investment of Gems later to unlock Gear 5. |

### The Golden Rule of Experience Boosts

Do not waste your 2x EXP boosts the moment you get them. Experienced players recommend grinding manually without any boosts until you reach **Level 175**. Once you hit this milestone, activate your 2x EXP boosts to breeze through the mid-game grind. Saving a portion of your boosts for the late-game levels (past Level 1000) will also save you dozens of hours of repetitive farming.

## Essential Early-Game Items and Progression Milestones

To maximize your damage and survivability, you need to collect specific accessories and unlock crucial utility skills as early as possible. Do not spend your hard-earned money on random spins early on; save your cash for guaranteed upgrades.

### Key Drops and Abilities to Acquire

1. **Pearl Necklace**: Drop from Ace, the raid boss at the Jungle Island. This boss has a 100% drop rate for this item, making it an essential target. It boosts your fruit damage by 7.5% and provides a substantial health pool increase.
2. **Buso Haki (Armament)**: Head over to Logue Town to purchase this. It allows your physical attacks to damage Logia-type bosses (such as the Ice Admiral) who are otherwise immune to basic physical strikes.
3. **Observation Haki**: Located at the Marine Base (Smoke Boss Island). This skill allows you to see boss and player health bars through walls and grants you automatic dodges to completely evade high-damage attacks.

## The Third Sea Update: End-Game Content Preview

Once you conquer the first two seas, a massive amount of new content awaits you in the Third Sea. This update introduces powerful new fruits, upgraded fighting styles, and devastating weapons.

### The Mythical Okuchi Fruit

The **Okuchi Fruit** is one of the most sought-after additions in the game. It completely revamps your character's movement animations, giving you custom dashes, a unique Geppo (sky jump), and custom M1 basic attacks. 

Its skills include:
* **Feral Pursuit (Z-Move)**: A rapid forward dash that grabs and slams your target.
* **Frozen Flame (X-Move)**: Launches a long-range orb of icy fire that explodes on impact.
* **Cold Breath (C-Move)**: A highly controllable, continuous stream of frost that can hit multiple targets at extreme ranges.
* **Transformation (E-Move)**: Turn into a majestic mythical beast, enhancing all your base skills and unlocking the devastating **Blistering Fury (V-Move)** which applies a damage-over-time burn effect.

### Upgrading to Fishman V2

For players who rely on Devil Fruits, touching ocean water is usually fatal. However, unlocking **Fishman V2** completely changes the game. 

By upgrading your race, your character swaps their fins for a functional fish tail. This grants you **complete water immunity**, meaning you will no longer take damage in the sea even if you are a Devil Fruit user. Additionally, you receive a temporary speed boost for 4 seconds whenever you exit the water.

### Crafting Legendary Gear at the Blacksmith

The Third Sea features a dedicated Blacksmith NPC who can craft high-tier armor and weapons using materials dropped by Sea Beasts.

| Crafted Item | Required Materials | Key Benefits & Buffs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sea Beast Helmet V2** | Sea Beast Scales, Iron Ore, Beli | Massive health pool boost and elemental damage reduction. |
| **Sea Beast Armor V2** | Sea Beast Core, Scales, Beli | High defense scaling and health regeneration. |
| **Three Sword Style V2 (3SS V2)** | 3SS V1, Ancient Blade Drill, Mastery 300+ | Custom M1 sword slash animations and massive AoE skills like *Purgatory Descent*. |

To obtain the base Three Sword Style (V1), players must locate a hidden sword shrine on a specific rock formation while possessing the Shusui, Enma, and Wado Ichimonji (Zeled) swords at 300+ mastery.

## Advanced Tips for Aspiring Masters

To wrap up this **haze seas beginner guide**, let's look at some advanced strategies shared by top-tier players on the [Roblox Platform](https://www.roblox.com) to help you transition smoothly into the end-game:

* **Super Boss Farming**: Don't sleep on the Ice Admiral and Smoke bosses. They each have a 5% chance to drop Super Boss spawn items. Group up with other players to summon and defeat these Super Bosses quickly for rare gems and materials.
* **Gem Hoarding**: If your ultimate goal is to become a "Gum Main" and unlock Gear 5, start saving your Gems immediately. Gear 5 requires an astronomical 150 Gems per ability, across 7 distinct abilities. Do not waste Gems on temporary stat resets or mid-tier items.
* **Bounty Spawning**: If you manage to accumulate 25 Million Bounty, you unlock the exclusive **Demon Spawner** item. This allows you to summon massive Ores (giant NPCs similar to Sea Beasts) that will aggressively hunt down other players in your server.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best fruit for beginners in Haze Seas?
According to this **haze seas beginner guide**, the best fruit for starting out is the **Phoenix** fruit due to its incredible healing abilities and solid AoE attacks. If you cannot roll a Phoenix, any Elemental (Logia) fruit like **Ice** or **Sand** is highly recommended because they make you immune to damage from lower-level enemies.

### How do I get water immunity if I have a Devil Fruit?
To get complete water immunity, you need to upgrade your race to **Fishman V2** (Fishman Born V2). This replaces your fins with a fish tail, removes all water damage, and gives you a temporary speed boost when exiting the ocean.

### Where can I find the Blacksmith to craft the Sea Beast gear?
The Blacksmith is located in the Third Sea. You can visit him to craft powerful end-game gear like the Sea Beast Helmet V2, Sea Beast Armor V2, and the highly coveted Three Sword Style V2 weapon.
