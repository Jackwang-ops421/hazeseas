## Factions, Races, and Character Creation

When you first spawn into the game, you must choose between two main factions: Pirates and Marines. Your faction choice is not permanent and can be changed later, but it dictates how you interact with other players. According to community reports, playing as a Marine has a unique mechanic where faction members can hit each other, making it a popular choice for friends who want to engage in friendly sparring matches. 

Your character's race is arguably more important than your faction. Every race grants passive buffs that alter your combat style. The most coveted race is the D Clan, which grants access to a highly powerful ability known as Conqueror's Haki.

| Race | Key Buffs & Abilities | Special Requirements |

If you are a returning player, the developers do not automatically reset your progress. You will keep your level, fruits, fighting styles, and inventory. However, if you want to experience the game from scratch, you can visit the Level 1 Starter Island. Head inside the tavern and locate the reset button on the wall. ## Unlocking Essential Movement Skills

While this **haze seas leveling guide** focuses on progression, unlocking movement abilities is equally critical. Navigating the islands quickly reduces your travel time, allowing you to complete quests faster. The two most important movement skills are Flash Step and Sky Walk.

To unlock Flash Step, which allows you to teleport short distances instantly by pressing the **T** key, head to Clown Island (Level 40+). Locate the Flash Step NPC in the town and pay the required fee to learn the skill.

To unlock Sky Walk, which allows you to jump repeatedly through the air, return to the Starter Island. Look for the massive stone pillars rising out of the water nearby. At the base of one of these pillars, you will find a hidden cave entrance. Head inside, climb to the top of the platforms, and speak to the Blackleg NPC. He will teach you the Sky Walk ability, which can be upgraded later for extra jumps.

Our **haze seas leveling guide** recommends prioritizing stamina upgrades early on if you plan to use these mobility tools constantly. Both Flash Step and Sky Walk consume stamina, and running out of it mid-air can leave you vulnerable to enemy attacks or drown you in the ocean.

## Mastering Haki: Armament, Observation, and Conqueror's

To survive the high-level islands, you must unlock Haki. These specialized combat techniques allow you to bypass the elemental defenses of certain Devil Fruit users and boost your overall damage output. This **haze seas leveling guide** outlines the exact locations of these crucial combat upgrades.

### Busoshoku Haki (Armament)
Busoshoku Haki coats your limbs and weapons in a dark armor, increasing your defensive capabilities and physical damage. 
* **Location:** Loguetown (Level 350 Island)
* **NPC:** Old Man Silver (Rayleigh)
* **Activation Key:** Press **J**

### Observation Haki
Observation Haki allows you to dodge incoming attacks automatically and view the health bars and levels of nearby players and NPCs.
* **Location:** Level 600 Island
* **NPC:** Observation Trainer
* **Activation Key:** Press **R**
* **Training Method:** Keep Observation Haki active and allow enemies to attack you. Each successful dodge levels up the skill, increasing the number of dodges you can perform before the ability goes on cooldown.

### Conqueror's Haki
As mentioned earlier, Conqueror's Haki is a rare ability exclusive to members of the D Clan race. When activated by pressing the **H** key, it unleashes a powerful shockwave that stuns surrounding enemies. If two players use Conqueror's Haki near each other, it triggers a clashing animation that showcases their spiritual pressure.

## Finding Secret Mounts

If you want to travel across land quickly without consuming stamina, acquiring a mount is your best option. Players can obtain a secret Dinosaur Mount by traveling to Skull Island. 

Once you arrive at Skull Island, search for an NPC named Vanny. By speaking to her, you can unlock the ability to purchase the Dino Mount. After interacting with Vanny, open your inventory, navigate to the items tab, and buy the mount. Once purchased, you can summon the dinosaur at any time to ride across the islands in style.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the built-in haze seas leveling guide work?
The game features an automated progression system. Once your character reaches the minimum level requirement for a new island, the map UI will display a marker directing you to the next zone. This guide ensures you are always fighting enemies that yield optimal experience points.

### Where can I find the reset button if I want to restart my character?
To reset your levels, stats, and Devil Fruits permanently, head to the tavern located on the Level 1 Starter Island. Inside, you will find a physical reset button on the wall that clears your progress.

### How do I train and level up my Observation Haki?
You train Observation Haki by activating it with the **R** key and letting enemies attack you. Every auto-dodge you perform contributes experience points to your Observation Haki level, allowing you to dodge more attacks in the future.
