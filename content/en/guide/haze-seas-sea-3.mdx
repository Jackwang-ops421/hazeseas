## Preparing for the Journey: Sea 2 Progression and Requirements

Before you can even think about entering the third sea, you must complete your journey through the Second Sea. The Second Sea consists of 11 distinct islands, each tailored to specific level brackets. To stand a chance against the gatekeeper boss, players should ideally max out their levels and optimize their builds on the highest-level islands.

Below is a breakdown of the late-game islands in the Second Sea that you must conquer to prepare your character for the transition:

| Island Name | Level Range | Key Activities & Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Winter Island | Level 3600 – 3750 | Mastery farming and basic gear upgrades |
| Foggy Castle | Level 3750 – 3900 | Mid-tier boss grinding and weapon collection |
| Venom Island | Level 3900 – 4050 | High-level quest completion and final stat optimization |

Once you have reached Level 4200, you are ready to seek out the Gatekeeper NPC. This NPC is located inside the main building at Flower Capital and serves as the final arbiter of your progression.

## How to Unlock Haze Seas Sea 3: The Red Emperor Boss Fight

Unlocking **haze seas sea 3** requires a test of pure individual skill. When you speak to the Gatekeeper, he will warn you of the path forward, stating that it is determined by one who has ruled the seas with unmatched power: the Red Emperor.

Accepting the quest triggers a cutscene and immediately teleports you into a solo arena. You must fight the Red Emperor alone, meaning you cannot rely on friends or party members to carry you through the encounter.

### Red Emperor Boss Fight Strategy

The Red Emperor hits incredibly hard, utilizing rapid sword strikes and area-of-effect (AoE) slash attacks that can deplete your health bar in seconds.

* **Recommended Skills:** Utilize high-mobility moves and ranged attacks to keep your distance. Skills like Feral Howl or high-damage elemental bursts are excellent for dealing safe damage.

Once you successfully deplete the Red Emperor's health bar, a final victory cutscene will play, officially granting you access to the third sea.

## Mastering the Three-Sword Style (3SS) V1 and V2

As you progress through **haze seas sea 3**, upgrading your weaponry becomes essential for dealing with higher-level enemies. The update introduces the highly anticipated Three-Sword Style (3SS) V2, which offers superior damage and flashier visual effects.

To obtain these styles, you must follow a multi-step progression path involving specific swords, masteries, and rare materials.

### Obtaining Three-Sword Style V1

To unlock the first version of the Three-Sword Style, you must obtain three specific swords: Sand, Enma, and Shisui. You must train all three swords to a minimum of 300 Mastery. Once this requirement is met, locate the hidden rock formation in the world where the style is taught to claim your V1 moveset.

### Upgrading to Three-Sword Style V2

To upgrade to V2, you must visit the NPC named Doryan the Forge. He will upgrade your style if you possess the necessary materials.

| Sword Style Version | Requirements | Move Set |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Three-Sword Style V1** | Sand, Enma, and Shisui at 300 Mastery | • Pound Phoenix<br />• Purgatory Onigiri<br />• 3,000 Worlds |
| **Three-Sword Style V2** | 3SS V1 at 300 Mastery + 5 Ancient Blade Relics | • Soaring Phoenix<br />• Purgatory Descent<br />• 3,000 Skies |

The V2 moveset features enhanced visual effects reminiscent of the Wano arc aesthetic. Soaring Phoenix fires massive, stylized energy slashes, Purgatory Descent delivers an explosive downward strike, and 3,000 Skies unleashes a flurry of cuts that shreds enemy defenses.

## The New Mythical Fruit: Okuchi-no-Makami

Many players exploring **haze seas sea 3** seek out the new mythical Okuchi fruit. Inspired by the legendary wolf-beast transformation, this mythical-tier fruit offers a versatile mix of crowd control, damage, and mobility.

The fruit features two distinct states: Base Form and Transformed Form. Transforming alters your character's appearance into a glowing wolf hybrid and enhances the properties of all your skills.

| Move Name | Base Form Effect | Transformed Form Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Feral Pursuit / Strike** | Grabs the target and slams them down. | Faster execution with increased impact radius. |
| **Frozen Flames** | Fires a burst of icy energy that damages targets. | Burns and freezes enemies simultaneously. |
| **Cold Breath** | Exhales a cone of freezing wind that slows targets. | Deals significantly higher damage and freezes targets solid. |
| **Seeking / Pursuing Serpent** | Launches a homing projectile followed by two quick slashes. | Launches faster projectiles with double the slash range. |

In the transformed state, your basic left-click (M1) attacks also become much faster and deal increased physical damage, making it an excellent choice for both player-versus-environment (PvE) boss farming and player-versus-player (PvP) combat.

## Quality of Life Features: Race and Fruit Storage

In addition to new zones and combat styles, the developers have introduced several highly requested quality-of-life features to streamline character management.

### The Archivist of Bloodline (Race Storage)

Located near the fruit store, the Archivist of Bloodline allows players to store their current race in a dedicated slot. This feature ensures that if you decide to spin for a new race, your old race remains saved and untouched. 
* Players can purchase up to five race storage slots.
* Each slot costs 1,000 gems to unlock.
* This is highly beneficial for players who like to swap between races like Mink, Fishman, or Cyborg depending on their current build.

### Fruit Storage and Dealers

A similar storage system exists for fruits, though unlocking these slots carries a higher gem cost. The third sea also features a dedicated Fruit Dealer NPC, allowing players to spin for new fruits directly within the new region. Additionally, the Sage of Combat NPC is situated nearby, offering quests to unlock and upgrade various fighting styles to their V2 variants.

### The Demon Minion Spawner

For high-level players looking to show off their achievements, a new summonable demon helper is available. According to player experience, you must accumulate a total of 25 million bounty to receive the demon spawner item. 

Once obtained, you can use the spawner to summon a massive, blob-like demon minion. While community testing suggests that the minion's combat AI is still being adjusted, it currently follows the player around as a prestigious companion.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What level is required to access the third sea?
To access the third sea, players must complete the progression of the Second Sea and reach Level 4200. Once you reach this level, you must defeat the Red Emperor boss in a solo trial to unlock the gateway.

### How do I obtain the Ancient Blade Relics for Three-Sword Style V2?
Ancient Blade Relics are rare drops obtained by defeating high-level bosses and participating in raids within the new region. You will need to collect five of these relics and have 300 Mastery on your V1 style to purchase the upgrade from Doryan the Forge.

### Can I store multiple races in the new update?
Yes. The Archivist of Bloodline NPC allows you to store your active race so you do not lose it when spinning for a new one. With the release of **haze seas sea 3**, players can utilize up to five distinct race storage slots, which can be unlocked for 1,000 gems each.
