## Prerequisites to Unlock Gear 5

Before you can summon ancient entities and wield the power of liberation, you must lay down the groundwork. The progression system in the game requires you to fully master the previous forms of the Rubber Fruit (historically known as the Gum-Gum Fruit) before transitioning to the final form. 

Unlocking **haze seas gear 5** requires a significant time investment. You must reach the maximum level cap designed for this gear tier, obtain specific progression items, and have previously unlocked both Gear 2 and Gear 4. 

| Requirement | Target Value / Status | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Character Level** | Level 3,000 | The absolute minimum level required to interact with the trainer NPC. |
| **Active Fruit** | Rubber Fruit (Gum-Gum) | Must be equipped during the boss fight and when speaking to the trainer. |
| **Prior Transformations** | Gear 2 & Gear 4 | You must have unlocked and mastered these previous forms. |
| **Gear 4 Book** | Mastered | Ensure your Gear 4 progression is fully completed. |

Failing to meet any of these baseline requirements will prevent you from progressing, even if you manage to obtain the necessary boss drops. Make sure your character is fully leveled and your inventory is prepared before moving on to the summoning phase.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Obtaining Gear 5

Once you have met the basic prerequisites, the path to obtaining **haze seas gear 5** involves a multi-stage boss hunt and a substantial currency payment. The entire process takes place across two major locations: Onigashima (also known as Dragon Island) and Skull Island.

### Step 1: Farm the Dragon Orb
Your first destination is Onigashima. Here, you must locate and repeatedly defeat the Mace V2 Boss. This boss has a highly coveted but exceptionally rare drop: the Dragon Orb. Player experiences indicate that this grind can take several hours due to the low drop rate.

### Step 2: Summon the Dragon Super Boss
Once you have successfully obtained the Dragon Orb, head to the summoning altar on Onigashima. Use the orb to summon the level 3,500 Dragon Super Boss. This is a challenging encounter, so bringing a group of high-level players is highly recommended.

### Step 3: Secure the Drums of Liberation
Defeat the Dragon Super Boss while keeping your Rubber Fruit equipped. If you successfully defeat the boss under these conditions, you are guaranteed a 100% drop rate for the Drums of Liberation. This item is the key to unlocking your final transformation.

### Step 4: Locate the Trainer NPC
With the Drums of Liberation in your inventory, travel to Skull Island. Look up to find the Floating Rock hovering directly above the island. Use a flying ability or climb to reach the top, where you will find the Gear 5 NPC Trainer named Incurr.

### Step 5: Pay the Awakening Fee
Speak to Incurr to finalize your transformation. In addition to handing over the Drums of Liberation, you must pay a hefty fee in Beli and Gems.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the exact requirements to get haze seas gear 5?
To unlock this form, you must be level 3,000, have Gear 2 and Gear 4 unlocked, and equip the Rubber Fruit. You then need to defeat the Mace V2 Boss on Onigashima for a Dragon Orb (1% drop rate), summon and defeat the Dragon Super Boss for the Drums of Liberation, and pay the NPC trainer Incurr $500,000 Beli and 100 Gems.

### Where can I find the Gear 5 NPC trainer?
The trainer NPC, named Incurr, is located on the Floating Rock. This landmass hovers directly above Skull Island. You will need a flying ability or a high-climbing method to reach him.

### Does the Dragon Super Boss always drop the Drums of Liberation?
Yes, the Dragon Super Boss has a 100% drop rate for the Drums of Liberation, but only if you have the Rubber Fruit equipped during the battle. If you defeat the boss with a different fruit active, the item will not drop.

### Is Gear 5 viable for PvP combat?
According to player experience and community reports, the transformation is highly viable for PvP. The combination of the *Spiritual Dominance* stun, the wide range of *God Clap*, and the mobility of *Cloud Fly* allows players to control the pace of battles effectively.
