## Step-by-Step Guide to the Second Sea Quest

Once you have reached Level 2200, the actual quest to unlock the new world begins. The quest is relatively straightforward, but it requires you to travel across the map to retrieve a specific historical artifact.

### Step 1: Talk to the Dimensional Sailor
Your first destination is **Starter Island**, the very place where your journey began. Look around the docks or the shoreline to locate the **Dimensional Sailor** NPC. When you interact with him, he will inform you that he can transport you to a brand-new world, but only if you prove your worth by bringing him the **Poneglyph Scroll**.

### Step 2: Navigate to the Rocky Pillar
After accepting the quest, you need to set sail for the **Rocky Pillar**. This is a distinct rock formation located out in the ocean. According to player experience, it is best to use a fast boat to reach this location quickly, as the open waters can sometimes be hazardous.

### Step 3: Trigger the Cutscene and Grab the Scroll
As soon as your boat pulls up to the Rocky Pillar, a cutscene will automatically trigger. Watch the cutscene to learn more about the lore of the world. Once the scene ends, search the immediate area for a large, **glowing cube**. Interact with this cube to receive the Poneglyph Scroll in your inventory.

### Step 4: Return and Set Sail
With the Poneglyph Scroll safely in your possession, make your way back to Starter Island. Speak with the Dimensional Sailor once again. He will accept the scroll, open a portal, and teleport you directly to the **haze seas second sea**. Congratulations, you have officially unlocked the next phase of the game!

## Second Sea Island Progression & Level Ranges

Once you have gained access to the **haze seas second sea**, the game structure changes. The environment is divided into several distinct islands, each tailored to specific level brackets. To level up efficiently and avoid getting defeated by high-level mobs, you should follow the recommended island progression pathway.

Community reports indicate that there are a total of 11 islands in this sea, though some are hidden or reserved for future expansions. Below is the verified progression list for the primary leveling zones.

### Flower Capital (Level 2200 - 2400)
This is your entry point into the new world. The Flower Capital features beautiful cherry blossoms and traditional architecture. Here, you will fight basic enemies to get acclimated to the increased difficulty of the second sea. Focus on gathering quests from local NPCs to bridge the gap to the next island.

### Udon Prison (Level 2400 - 2550)
A bleak, heavily guarded industrial prison island. The enemies here have high defense stats, so make sure your stat points are properly distributed into your primary damage-dealing attributes (Sword, Fruit, or Melee).

### Shadow Island (Level 3000 - 3150)
You will notice a significant level gap between Udon Prison and Shadow Island. According to player experiences, you may need to farm bosses or participate in raids to bridge this gap before safely tackling Shadow Island. The enemies here utilize dark elemental attacks.

### Peanut Island (Level 3150 - 3300)
The beginning of the sweet-themed archipelago. The mobs here are closely packed together, making them excellent targets for Area of Effect (AoE) attacks.

### Dough Island (Level 3300 - 3450)
Dough Island features challenging mini-bosses that drop valuable crafting materials. Make sure to set your spawn point at the local dock before fighting these enemies.

### Cake Island (Level 3450 - 3600)
A massive confectionery-themed island. The quests here offer substantial experience points, allowing you to quickly push toward the endgame level brackets.

### Foggy Castle (Level 3600 - 3750)
A mysterious castle shrouded in dense fog. Visibility is low, and the enemies are highly aggressive. Having a detection ability or a high-mobility fruit is highly recommended here.

### Winter Island (Level 3750 - 3900)
The current peak leveling zone in this region. The frozen environment is home to some of the strongest enemies in the game, requiring optimized builds and high-tier gear to survive.

## Essential Tips for Surviving the Second Sea

The transition to the new map can be jarring for players who cruised through the early game. To ensure you do not spend all your time running back from spawn points, keep these essential survival tips in mind.

### Set Your Spawn Point Immediately
Every time you land on a new island, your very first action should be to locate the Spawn Point NPC and set your home location. The enemies in the **haze seas second sea** can easily defeat unprepared players in just a few hits. Setting your spawn point saves you from long, tedious boat rides back from the Flower Capital.

### Utilize Shipwrights
Navigating the **haze seas second sea** requires a reliable boat. Fortunately, most islands feature docks with a local **Shipwright** NPC. These vendors sell a variety of boats ranging from cheap, basic dinghies to high-speed luxury vessels. Investing in a fast boat is crucial for avoiding sea beasts and traveling between distant islands efficiently.

### Optimize Your Stats and Build
Before making the jump to the **haze seas second sea**, review your character build. If your stats are spread too thinly across Strength, Defense, Sword, and Fruit, you may find your damage output lacking. Focus on one primary offensive stat and pair it with a healthy amount of Defense to survive the high-damage attacks of Second Sea bosses.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stat Reset** | Optimizes damage output | Focus points into one main attack stat (e.g., Fruit or Sword) |
| **Buy a Fast Boat** | Saves travel time, avoids hazards | Purchase from a Shipwright at any major dock |
| **Set Spawn Points** | Prevents losing progress upon death | Find the spawn NPC immediately upon landing on a new island |
| **Upgrade Accessories** | Provides passive stat boosts | Farm First Sea bosses for rare drops before leaving |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I get back to the first sea from the haze seas second sea?
You can return to the First Sea by speaking to the Dimensional Sailor NPC located at the main docks of the Flower Capital. He will offer to teleport you back to the Starter Island of the First Sea free of charge.

### What is the level cap in the haze seas second sea?
Based on the current island progression, the level cap extends up to Level 3900 at Winter Island. However, community reports and game updates frequently raise this cap as new islands and bosses are introduced.

### Do my items and fruits transfer to the second sea?
Yes, all of your levels, statistics, inventory items, weapons, and equipped fruits transfer with you. You will not lose any of your progress when making the transition to the new map.

### Can I skip the level 2200 requirement if a friend invites me?
No, the level requirement is a hard lock built into the quest system. Even if you join a friend's server, you cannot interact with the Dimensional Sailor or access the portals until your character reaches Level 2200.
