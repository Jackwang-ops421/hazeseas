To successfully set up an AFK farm, your character needs to meet specific combat requirements. Without these, your character will likely be defeated by the bosses or fail to deal damage to specific enemy types.

Buso Haki is non-negotiable for mid-to-late-game grinding. Without it, you cannot damage Logia-type users, which will completely halt your AFK progress if you encounter them. Observation Haki is equally important as it allows your character to automatically dodge incoming attacks, ensuring your survival over hours of unattended farming.

### 2. The Right Devil Fruit
While you can technically use basic M1 attacks for grinding, utilizing a Devil Fruit with rapid cooldowns and high area-of-effect (AoE) damage speeds up the process dramatically. According to player experience, the **Dough Fruit** is highly favored for AFK setups due to its fast cooldowns and high damage output. Other viable options include Logia-type fruits like Light or Flame, which grant passive immunity to non-Haki-using enemies.

If you want your AFK setup to succeed, your stats must be optimized. Spreading your points too thin across all categories will lower your damage output and slow your progression. Focus on a single primary damage type and keep Defense as your secondary priority.

## Safety and Best Practices for AFK Farming

While AFK grinding is incredibly helpful, there are a few rules of thumb you should follow to prevent your character from dying or disconnecting:

1.  **Private Servers:** If possible, execute your AFK macros on a private server. This prevents other players from dragging your character away, killing you in PvP, or stealing your boss spawns.
2.  **Monitor Your Temperature:** Running Roblox and macro tools for hours can heat up your PC. Make sure your computer has adequate cooling.
3.  **Check Your Inventory:** Ensure your inventory isn't full if you are farming bosses that drop rare swords or accessories.
4.  **Test the Loop First:** Don't leave your computer immediately after starting the macro. Watch it run for at least 10 to 15 minutes to ensure your character doesn't slowly drift out of position due to knockback.
