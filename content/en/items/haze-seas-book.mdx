## Understanding the Haze Seas Book Utility

The **haze seas book** is a unique item that serves a specific purpose in your character's growth. According to community reports, this item is primarily used to unlock the 2SS v2 (Two Sword Style version 2). If you are looking to refine your combat abilities, obtaining this item is a mandatory milestone.

### Steps to Use the Book
1. **Purchase the Item:** You can buy the book for 20,000 Beli.
2. **Equip the Item:** Ensure the book is active in your inventory.
3. **Locate the Scientist:** Travel to Logue Town.
4. **Interact:** Speak with the white-clad scientist to finalize the unlock process.

| Requirement | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Item Name** | Book |
| **Primary Use** | Unlocking 2SS v2 |

## The Hunt for the Three Sword Style

While the book is vital for 2SS, many players are currently grinding for the highly anticipated Three Sword Style. Based on player experience, this style is no longer obtained from the old Zoro boss. Instead, you must gather three specific legendary swords before heading to the Third Sea.

### Required Swords for the Three Sword Style
To master this technique, you must secure the following weapons and reach 300 mastery on each:

*   **Shusui:** Dropped by the Ryuma boss located in the First Sea.
*   **Zenith:** Obtained by defeating the boss on the Level 3,000 Shadow Island in the Second Sea.
*   **Enma:** A rare drop (10%) from the boss in the Flower Capital mansion in the Second Sea.

### Boss Drop Statistics
Knowing your odds helps manage your grinding time effectively. Here is a breakdown of the bosses you will need to face.

| Boss Name | Location | Drop Item | Estimated Difficulty |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ryuma** | First Sea Island | Shusui | Moderate |
| **Zenith Boss** | Shadow Island | Zenith Sword | High (189k HP) |
| **Enma Boss** | Flower Capital | Enma | Moderate |

## Optimal Grinding Strategy

Grinding for these items requires patience and a good plan. Since the Enma boss spawns only once every hour, it is wise to prioritize this boss while waiting for other event spawns. Community reports suggest that grouping up with other players can significantly decrease the time spent fighting high-HP bosses like the one on Shadow Island.

### Efficiency Tips for Players
*   **Server Hopping:** If the Enma boss isn't up, check different servers to see if it has spawned elsewhere.
*   **Mastery Farming:** Use your swords against low-level NPCs to reach the 300 mastery requirement quickly without risking your life against high-tier bosses.
*   **Stay Informed:** Keep an eye on the official [Haze Piece Wiki](https://haze-piece.fandom.com/wiki/Book) for any sudden changes to drop rates or spawn timers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Where do I go to use the haze seas book?**
A: You need to travel to Logue Town and speak with the white scientist. Ensure you have the book equipped before you attempt to interact with him.

A: No. The book is specifically for the 2SS v2 style. The Three Sword Style requires collecting Shusui, Zenith, and Enma, and hitting 300 mastery on each.

**Q: How often does the Enma boss spawn?**
A: Based on current player reports, the Enma boss spawns in the Flower Capital mansion once every hour.

**Q: Is the 20,000 Beli cost for the book permanent?**
A: Yes, that is the standard price. Make sure you have enough currency saved up before heading to Logue Town to avoid unnecessary trips.
