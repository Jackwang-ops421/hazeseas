To find the Sea Beast and begin your hunt for the **haze seas sea beast core**, you must navigate to the Rocky Pillars. This location is distinct and relatively easy to spot from a distance due to the towering stone structures rising out of the ocean.

## Mastering the Sea Beast Attack Patterns

Success in Haze Seas combat relies heavily on telegraphing. The Sea Beast follows a strict consecutive attack pattern, moving from its first move to its third in a predictable cycle. By memorizing these moves, you can minimize the damage you take and maximize your uptime for dealing damage.

### The Attack Cycle (A to C)

1.  **Aquatic Fireball:** The beast launches a massive blue fireball. While the windup is very obvious, the hitbox is surprisingly large. If you are too close, dodging can be difficult. This move deals approximately 2,500 damage.
2.  **Roar:** The beast lets out a deafening cry. While this move deals no direct damage, it is perhaps the most dangerous part of the fight. The Roar acts as a stun and, more importantly, it disables your Observation Haki.
3.  **Sea Beam:** A fast-moving blue laser with incredible range. This move deals 1,500 damage. Because it follows the Roar, you must be careful; if your Haki is disabled and you are stunned, the Sea Beam is almost guaranteed to hit.

### Strategy for Survival
Player experience suggests that the best way to handle the Roar is to anticipate the timing. Since the attacks go in order (Fireball -> Roar -> Beam), you should prepare to dodge immediately after the Fireball ends. Once the Roar is finished, quickly re-enable your Observation Haki to ensure you can see the Sea Beam coming.

## Farming Tips and the "Cheese" Strategy

If you find yourself struggling with the Sea Beast’s high damage output, there is a community-reported "cheese" method that can make the fight significantly easier. 

### The Pillar Method
Near the spawn area of the Sea Beast, there is a specific pillar that houses the Sky Walk Trainer. Players have discovered that if you stand on top of this pillar, you are largely out of reach of the Sea Beast’s melee range and some of its larger hitboxes. From this vantage point, you can spam ranged moves (such as Fruit abilities or Gun shots) to whittle down the boss's health in relative safety.

### Farming Checklist
To maximize your efficiency when hunting for the **haze seas sea beast core**, follow this checklist:
*   **Bring a Team:** Even if you can solo it, a team of three or four can clear the boss in a fraction of the time.
*   **Set Spawn:** Ensure your spawn point is set nearby so a single mistake doesn't result in a long boat ride back.
*   **Damage Over Everything:** The Sea Beast’s attacks are telegraphed. If you can dodge well, you don't need much health; focus your stat points on your primary damage source (Fruit, Sword, or Melee).
*   **Server Hopping:** If the boss is on cooldown, consider switching servers to find a fresh spawn.
