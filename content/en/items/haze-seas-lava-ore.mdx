## Locating the Level 600 Island and the Base Boss

To begin your hunt for the **haze seas lava ore**, you must set sail for the Level 600 Island. This island is designed for players who have already put a significant amount of time into leveling up their stats and masteries. If you attempt this island at a lower level, you will likely find the enemies and the local boss far too difficult to defeat efficiently.

Once you arrive at the Level 600 Island, your primary target is the local island boss. This boss spawns in a designated arena on the island and is the sole source of the raw ore. According to player experience and community reports, the drop rate for this item is exceptionally low, meaning you will need to prepare for a prolonged farming session.

| Boss Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | Level 600 Island |
| **Recommended Level** | 600+ |
| **Target Boss** | Level 600 Island Boss |
| **Primary Drop** | **Haze seas lava ore** |

Because of the 2.5% drop rate, it is highly recommended to bring friends or join a public server where other players are actively farming the boss. This speeds up the defeat time and allows you to get more attempts per hour.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Farming the Lava Ore

Farming a 2.5% drop can be a test of patience. To maximize your efficiency while hunting for the **haze seas lava ore**, you should optimize your combat build and server setup. Using high-damage fruits or sword styles with wide area-of-effect (AoE) moves will help you melt the boss's health bar quickly.

Additionally, setting your spawn point directly on the Level 600 Island is crucial. This minimizes downtime if you happen to get defeated during the battle. 

| Farming Strategy | Pros | Cons |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Private Server Farming** | No interference from other players; boss spawns consistently for you. | You must defeat the boss entirely on your own, which takes longer. |
| **Public Server Hopping** | You can find servers where the boss is already spawned or half-defeated. | High competition for drops and potential lag. |
| **Group Farming** | Fastest kill times; shared aggro makes the fight much safer. | Drops are individual, so some group members might get it before others. |

If you are playing solo, utilizing Observation Haki (often referred to as Observation) is incredibly helpful. This allows you to dodge the boss's heavy attacks while continuously dealing damage. Keep your distance, cycle through your cooldowns, and repeat the process until the item finally drops into your inventory.

## Crafting the Lava Key and Summoning the Secret Boss

Obtaining the **haze seas lava ore** is only the first part of the journey. Once the ore is safely in your inventory, you must use it to summon the secret Lava Boss. 

First, locate the stone table situated on the Level 600 Island. When you have the ore in your possession, you can approach this table to interact with it and obtain the Lava Key. Note that you cannot pick up this key unless you have the ore; the game requires the ore as a prerequisite to summon the trial.

Once you have the Lava Key, follow these steps to summon the boss:

1. Locate the underground entrance or cave trial area on the Level 600 Island.
2. Approach the locked gate and interact with the key slot.
3. Use the Lava Key to open the gate and trigger the boss spawn.
4. Prepare for immediate combat as the boss summons into the arena.

| Summoning Step | Location | Requirement | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1. Acquire Ore | Level 600 Boss Arena | Defeat the level 600 boss | Pick up the 2.5% drop |
| 2. Get Lava Key | Stone Table (Island Surface) | Have **haze seas lava ore** in inventory | Interact with the table |
| 3. Find Gate | Underground Cave | Lava Key in hand | Interact with the gate lock |
| 4. Summon Boss | Trial Arena | Unlock the gate | Defeat the summoned boss |

## Defeating the Lava Boss: Combat Strategies

The summoned Lava Boss is a formidable opponent that deals massive damage. Players who attempt this fight unprepared will quickly find themselves sent back to their spawn point. Fortunately, the boss arena provides some room to maneuver, allowing you to dodge incoming attacks if you watch the boss's animations closely.

If you are running a sword build, such as the popular Two-Sword Style, you will want to get close to deal damage but quickly back away when the boss winds up its heavy attacks. Using your Observation ability is highly recommended here to auto-dodge dangerous strikes. 

If you are carrying a lower-level friend through the fight, have them stay back and offer support with ranged fruit abilities while you draw the boss's aggro. The boss has a relatively large health pool, but with sustained damage and careful dodging, it can be defeated in a few minutes.

## FAQ

### Where do I get the haze seas lava ore?
You can get the ore by defeating the boss on the Level 600 Island. It has an estimated drop rate of 2.5%, so you may need to defeat the boss multiple times before it drops.

### Can I summon the Lava Boss without the Lava Ore?
No. You must have the ore in your inventory to pick up the Lava Key from the table. Without the Lava Key, the trial gate will not open, and the boss will not summon.

### What are the stats of the Coded Mask?
The Coded Mask provides a massive 55% boost to all sword damage and grants an additional 500 health to your character, making it an elite accessory for sword builds.

### Is the Mace a guaranteed drop from the secret boss?
Yes, community reports and player experiences confirm that the Mace has a 100% drop rate upon defeating the summoned Lava Boss.
