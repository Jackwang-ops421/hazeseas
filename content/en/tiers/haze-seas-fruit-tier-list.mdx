S-Tier fruits are the best of the best in Haze Seas. These options boast incredible skill scaling, massive AoE damage, and unrivaled mobility tools. Our community-backed **haze seas fruit tier list** places these options at the top because they carry progression effortlessly.

### Dragon
The Dragon fruit remains the undisputed king of the game. It allows players to transform into a massive mythical dragon, granting flight, immense damage resistance, and a lethal arsenal of attacks. Moves like Blast Breath and Violet Rain decimate large groups of enemies instantly, making it the premier choice for late-game content.

### Electricity
Electricity is currently considered one of the most overpowered additions to the Haze Seas roster. Testers note that its transformed state allows players to fly while raining down high-damage, long-range attacks. This lets you stay completely out of reach of ground-based enemies while melting their health bars.

### Dough & Gum (Gomu)
Dough is widely regarded as the ultimate farming fruit due to its heavy AoE and crowd-control abilities. Meanwhile, Gum (Luffy's signature fruit) offers incredible versatility. By unlocking different "Gears," players can shift their stats to prioritize speed, power, or mobility on the fly.
