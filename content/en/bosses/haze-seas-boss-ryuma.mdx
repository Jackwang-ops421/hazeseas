## Where to Find the Haze Seas Boss Ryuma

Finding the **haze seas boss ryuma** can be slightly tricky if you do not know the layout of Thriller Boat. The island is dark, maze-like, and filled with aggressive enemies. 

To locate Ryummy, head to the middle section of the Thriller Boat island. Look for the massive stone wall structure that runs through the center of the zone. Follow this wall further up and toward the interior of the island. You will find Ryummy standing directly alongside the wall, waiting for players to challenge him. 

### Step-by-Step Navigation to Ryummy

1. **Dock at Thriller Boat:** Ensure your character is sufficiently leveled (ideally around level 2100+) to survive the journey.
2. **Locate the Center Wall:** Head past the initial graveyard and zombie spawns toward the towering stone walls in the middle of the island.
3. **Follow the Wall Upward:** Travel along the elevated pathways adjacent to the wall.
4. **Locate the Spawn Platform:** Ryummy spawns in a designated alcove right next to the wall. If he is not there, check his respawn timer or look for other players who might have recently defeated him.

## Strategies for Defeating Haze Seas Boss Ryuma

At level 2150, Ryummy hits incredibly hard. If you try to stand toe-to-toe with him without a plan, his high-damage sword skills can quickly deplete your health bar. Here are some proven strategies from top-tier players to help you defeat the **haze seas boss ryuma** efficiently:

### 1. Keep Your Distance with Fruit Powers
If you are a Fruit user (such as Magma, Light, or Ice), use your ranged projectiles to chip away at Ryummy's health. He is a melee-focused boss, meaning he must get close to deal maximum damage. Use your dashes and flight abilities to stay out of his reach.

### 2. Utilize the Wall (The "Cheese" Method)
Community reports indicate that Ryummy can easily get stuck on the geometry of the Thriller Boat wall. By positioning yourself on the elevated ledge above him or behind the corner of the wall, you can hit him with area-of-effect (AoE) attacks while remaining completely safe from his Thrust Barrage.

### 3. Maximize Sword Defense
If you are a Sword main attempting to fight him with melee weapons, make sure you equip accessories that boost your health and sword defense. Wearing the Blue Scarf (if you manage to drop it early) or other defensive capes will greatly reduce the damage you take from his Star Impact.

### 4. Group Up with Friends
Farming a 5% drop rate can take time. Bringing a friend or joining a public grinding server allows you to defeat Ryummy much faster. Since drops are calculated individually for players who contribute enough damage, grouping up increases everyone's efficiency.
