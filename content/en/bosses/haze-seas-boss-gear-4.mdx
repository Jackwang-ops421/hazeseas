Defeating this boss is the only way to obtain the coveted **Gear 4 Book**, which has a notoriously low drop rate of just 0.5%. Players must prepare for a grind, as you will need to defeat him multiple times to unlock the complete Gear 4th move set.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Unlocking Gear 4th

Unlocking Gear 4th is a multi-step process that spans across both the First Sea and Second Sea (C2). Follow these steps to ensure you do not miss a single requirement.

### Step 1: Obtain Gear 2nd
Before you can even think about Gear 4th, you must unlock Gear 2nd. Go to Bubble Island and look for the secret entrance. Speak to the Gear 2nd Trainer. You must have:
* At least $100,000 Beli
* Character Level 750+
* 300 Gum-Gum Fruit Mastery

### Step 2: Farm the haze seas boss gear 4 Drop
Head to the bottom-left of Bubble Island and challenge the Level 2,000 G4 Boss. Keep defeating him until he drops the Gear 4 Book. According to player experiences, this can take anywhere from a few attempts to several hours due to the 0.5% drop rate.

### Step 3: Travel to the Second Sea (C2)
Once you have the book, travel to the Second Sea. You must reach at least Level 2,200 to interact with the Poneglyph required for the awakening ritual.

### Step 4: Interact with the Poneglyph
Locate the Poneglyph in the Second Sea. Interact with it, select "Gear Fourth" from the menu, and accept the transformation. A custom cinematic will play, and your character will begin to float, unlocking your first Gear 4th ability!

## Combat Strategy for the G4 Boss

Because the G4 Boss is Level 2,000, fighting him head-on without a plan will quickly lead to defeat. Here are some community-tested strategies to make the grind easier:

* **Keep Your Distance:** Use long-range fruit abilities (such as Light, Magma, or Ice) to chip away at his health while staying out of reach of his heavy physical attacks.
* **Enable Observation Haki:** This is crucial for dodging his high-damage skills automatically.
* **Server Hopping:** The boss has a spawn timer of approximately 20 seconds. However, if you are farming with a group, server hopping can sometimes speed up the process of finding an active boss.

## Beyond Gear 4: Preparing for Gear 5

Once you have mastered the **haze seas boss gear 4** farming route and unlocked all five skills, your ultimate goal will be Gear 5th. 

To transition from Gear 4th to Gear 5th, you must summon and defeat the Kaido raid boss. This requires defeating 1,000 NPC souls on the designated high-level island to spawn him. Defeating Kaido yields the **Drums of Liberation**, which you can take back to the Second Sea Poneglyph to awaken the god-like powers of Gear 5th.

## FAQ

### Where can I find the haze seas boss gear 4?
The G4 Boss is located on the bottom-left corner of Bubble Island (Sabaody). He is a Level 2,000 boss, so make sure you are properly leveled and equipped before initiating the fight.

### What is the drop rate of the Gear 4 Book?
The Gear 4 Book has a 0.5% drop rate from the G4 Boss. Because of this low rate, you will likely need to defeat the boss dozens of times to secure your first book.

### How many books do I need to fully max out Gear 4th?
You need a total of 5 Gear 4 Books. Each book you bring to the Poneglyph in the Second Sea unlocks a new skill, starting with Kong Gun and ending with the devastating King Kong Gun.

### Can I skip Gear 4th and go straight to Gear 5th?
No, you must follow the natural progression of the Gum-Gum Fruit. You must unlock Gear 2nd, farm the **haze seas boss gear 4** drops to complete Gear 4th, and then obtain the Drums of Liberation from Kaido to unlock Gear 5th.
